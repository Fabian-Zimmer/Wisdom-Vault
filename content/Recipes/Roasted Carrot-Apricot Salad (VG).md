## Instructions
**Note:** The chamoy can be kept in the fridge for up to a week. The carrots can be roasted and dressed the day before, then finished with herbs, apricots, and nuts just before serving.

1. Preheat oven to ~220°C fan.
2. In a large bowl, toss the carrots (1kg) with olive oil (4 tbsp), maple syrup (3 tbsp), salt (1 tsp), and a good grind of black pepper (two batches with 1/2 ingredients each is helpful). Spread across two large parchment-lined baking trays. Roast for ~20-25 min., tossing and swapping the trays halfway through, until nicely browned but still retaining a bite. Two batches/rounds if only one baking tray available.
3. While the carrots roast, make the chamoy: blend dried apricots (40g), maple syrup (1 tsp), sumac (2 tsp), lime juice (45ml), chilli flakes (1/2 tsp), garlic (1 clove), olive oil (2 tbsp), and salt (1/4 tsp) in the food processor (small bowl and knife blade) until a smooth paste forms.
4. As soon as the carrots are done, transfer to a large bowl with the chamoy and mix well. Leave for 20 min. for the flavours to come together.
5. Make the serving topping: chopped mint (10g), chopped dill (5g), finely sliced apricots (70g), and roughly chopped almonds (30g).
