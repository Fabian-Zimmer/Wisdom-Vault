## Ingredients
| Amount  | Name                   | Prep/Notes                         |
| ------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 166g    | Beetroots              | Pre-cooked                         |
| 166g    | Apples                 | Peeled, cored, roughly chopped     |
| 150g    | Onions                 | Finely chopped                     |
| 75g     | Raisins                | Finely chopped                     |
| 50g     | Brown sugar or jaggery | Original recipe uses 75g           |
| 100ml   | White wine vinegar     |                                    |
| 1/2 tsp | Mustard                |                                    |
| 1/4 tsp | Allspice berries       | Dried, whole-spice, goes in teabag |
| 1 tsp   | Black peppercorns      | Goes in teabag                     |
| 1/4 tsp | Salt                   |                                    |
**Note:** Makes about 350ml jar of volume.

## Instructions
**Note:** You will need a small teabag to hold the whole spices.
**Prep:** Beetroots have to be pre-cooked well (in aluminium foil, in oven at 210C for ~1h or more).

1. In a pot, sauté onions and raisins with salt (1/4 tsp) in a little oil until softened.
2. Add mustard (1/2 tsp) with a small splash of the white wine vinegar. Stir well.
3. Add the remaining vinegar (141ml) and all of the sugar (50g). Stir to combine.
4. Place the allspice berries and peppercorns into a teabag, close tightly, and add to the pot on a side, making sure you can remove it again later.
5. Cook gently until the sugar has fully dissolved.
6. Add the apple pieces and simmer for ~20 min. until soft.
7. Peel the cooked beetroots, weigh to 166g, then add them directly to the pot and cut into small pieces inside the pot — this avoids staining a chopping board.
8. Cook until the chutney has thickened to your liking.
9. Remove the spice teabag. Use a stick blender to blend to desired consistency — leave chunky or blend smooth.
