## Ingredients

### Pancakes
| Amount     | Name                                                  | Prep                                              |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 135g       | Plain flour                                           |                                                   |
| 60g        | Glutinous rice flour                                  | Not regular rice flour!                           |
| 325ml      | Ice-cold water                                        |                                                   |
| 1          | Flax egg (1 tbsp flaxseed powder + 3 tbsp warm water) | **Note:** Original was 1 normal egg               |
| 1.5 tbsp   | Gochujang chilli paste                                | Adjust to brand; Korean brands tend to be spicier |
| 5g         | Coriander                                             | Roughly chopped                                   |
| 5g         | (Long) red chilli                                     | Deseeded and finely chopped                       |
| 75ml       | Sunflower oil                                         | For frying                                        |
| 400g       | Asparagus                                             | Woody ends trimmed, spears halved lengthways      |
| 280g (6-7) | Spring onions                                         | Halved lengthways, then cut into ~8cm lengths     |

### Dipping Sauce
| Amount | Name              | Prep                            |
| ------ | ----------------- | ------------------------------- |
| 50ml   | Light soy sauce   |                                 |
| 1 tbsp | Honey             | Ideally runny rather than thick |
| 2 tsp  | Sesame seeds      | Toasted                         |
| 2 tsp  | Rice vinegar      |                                 |
| 1      | Garlic clove      | Crushed                         |
| 5g     | (Long) red chilli | Deseeded and finely chopped     |

## Instructions
**Note:** Makes ~4 pancakes.

1. Make the dipping sauce: whisk together all sauce ingredients — soy sauce (50ml), honey (1 tbsp), toasted sesame seeds (2 tsp), rice vinegar (2 tsp), crushed garlic (1 clove), and chopped chilli (5g) — in a small bowl. Set aside.
2. Put both flours (135g plain, 60g glutinous rice) into a large bowl and mix. 
3. In another bowl: add ice-cold water (325ml), flax egg (1, see prep notes above), gochujang (1.5 tbsp), coriander (5g), chilli (5g), and a pinch of salt. Whisk together, then slowly pour the wet ingredients into the well in the flour mixture. Whisk until just smooth — don't overmix.
4. Heat 1 tbsp of sunflower oil in a medium-sized non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Once hot, add a quarter of the asparagus and a quarter of the spring onions, all pointing in the same direction. Pour over ~140g (about a quarter) of the batter, spreading to cover the bottom of the pan. Cook for 2.5 min. before flipping, then cook for another 2.5 min. until crispy and golden. Transfer to a plate. Repeat to make 4 pancakes in total, adjusting heat and oil as you go.
5. Sprinkle chopped coriander and a little chilli. Serve with the dipping sauce alongside or lightly drizzled on top.
