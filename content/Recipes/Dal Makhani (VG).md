## Recommended Equipment
- Pressure cooker — for cooking the urad dal and kidney beans quickly
- Deep sauté pan, wok, or Dutch oven — for building the dal
- Small frying pan — for the tadka
- Food processor or high-speed blender — for cashew cream
- Tongs and a small steel or glass bowl — for the optional dhungar smoking method

## Instructions
**Notes:**
- Rinsing and scrubbing the beans removes some of the black colour, giving the finished dal its characteristic reddish-brown colour.
- Do not skip the initial high-heat boil for kidney beans — undercooking them can cause illness due to the lectin they contain.
- The dal tastes even better the next day.
- Stores 3-5 days in the fridge; freezes well for months. If freezing, add the tadka fresh when serving rather than before freezing.

### Prep
0. Rinse and scrub the urad dal (205g) and kidney beans (44g) several times until water runs clear.
	- *Day ahead or 8h ahead:* cover with cold water, add baking soda (1 tsp), and soak for 8h or overnight.
	- *Quick soak:* cover with boiling water and soak for 4h.
	Drain and rinse thoroughly after soaking.

### Cook the Lentils & Beans (pressure cooker)
1. Transfer the soaked, drained lentils and beans to the pressure cooker. Cover with 3-5cm of water and add salt (1/2 tsp). Heat on high until the first whistle, then reduce to medium heat and cook for another 3 whistles. Remove from heat and allow pressure to release naturally for ~10 min., then release remaining pressure manually before opening.
2. Drain over a bowl to reserve the cooking liquid. Top up with water to make 840-960ml (3.5-4 cups) total liquid. Roughly mash the lentils and beans with a potato masher, fork, or wooden spoon. Set both aside.

### Cashew Cream (make while the pressure cooker runs)
3. Blend soaked cashews (70g) with water (90ml), lemon juice (1 tbsp), and salt (1/2 tsp) in a blender or food processor for 3-4 min., scraping down the sides as needed, until completely smooth. *Note: double the recipe if using a high-speed blender, as the single batch is too small for most blender jars.*

### Cook the Dal
4. Heat oil (1.5 tbsp) in a deep sauté pan, wok, or Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the whole spices — cinnamon stick (1), cardamom seeds (4, only seeds), cloves (3), bay leaf (1), cumin seeds (1 tsp) — and cook for 45-60 sec., swirling frequently, until aromatic and sizzling.
5. Add the onion with a pinch of salt. Cook for 5-7 min., stirring occasionally, until starting to brown. Add a few splashes of water towards the end to deglaze and prevent overbrowning.
6. Add garlic (4 cloves) and ginger (1 inch piece) and cook for 1 min., stirring frequently.
7. Add the ground spices — nutmeg (1/8 tsp), coriander (1 tsp), red chilli powder (1 tsp), salt (1.5-2 tsp), and black pepper — and tomato paste (2 tbsp). Stir frequently for 60-90 sec.
8. Add the tomatoes and cook for 2-3 min. until softened and broken down.
9. Pour in the bean cooking liquid/water (~3.5-4 cups), scraping up any browned bits at the bottom of the pan. Add the mashed lentils and beans and stir well. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer.
10. Simmer for 60-80 min., stirring every ~10 min. Add freshly boiled water as needed if it starts to dry out. The longer it simmers, the creamier the texture.
11. Stir in the cashew cream or coconut milk (120ml) and simmer for a further 10 min., stirring occasionally.
	- *Optional:* for an even creamier result, run a stick blender through a third to half of the dal at this point.

### Dhungar Smoking (optional, can have health detriments)
12. Place a small steel or glass bowl in the centre of the dal. Using tongs, hold the charcoal over an open gas flame for 2-3 min., rotating occasionally, until red hot in spots with some white markings. Do not heat longer than 3 min. or the smoke will overpower the dal. Quickly transfer the charcoal to the bowl, drizzle oil (1/2 tsp) on top — it will smoke immediately — and cover the pan. Smoke for 2-3 min., then remove and discard the charcoal. 
    *No charcoal? Add 1/2-1 tsp smoked paprika with the ground spices in step 7 instead.*

### Finish
13. Stir in the chopped coriander (1 cup) and lemon juice (1 tbsp). Taste and adjust salt. If slightly too acidic, add sugar (1/2-1 tsp).
14. Fish out the cinnamon stick and bay leaf.
15. Make the tadka: melt vegan butter (42g) in a small frying pan over medium-high. Add ginger matchsticks (from 1 inch piece) if using and cook for 30 sec. Crush kasoori methi (1 tbsp) with your hands into the butter or add the powder form, then add garam masala (1 tsp) and red chilli powder (1/2 tsp). Swirl for 30 sec. Pour the tadka over the dal and stir in. Serve with basmati rice, naan, or rotis.

### Canned Kidney Bean Variant
Omit the kidney beans from the pressure cooker step. Cook only the urad dal (1-2 more whistles on medium heat, after the first whistle on high heat, is sufficient). Use 3/4-1 cup canned kidney beans, drained and roughly mashed, added together with the cooked urad dal and liquid in step 9.
