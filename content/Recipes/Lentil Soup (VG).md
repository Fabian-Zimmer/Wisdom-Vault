## Recommended Equipment
- Instant Pot or pressure cooker — or a Dutch oven/large soup pot for the stovetop variant
- Kitchen twine — for the bouquet garni
 
## Ingredients

| Amount          | Name                    | Prep                                                                                                                           |
| --------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 2 tbsp          | Extra virgin olive oil  |                                                                                                                                |
| 1 large         | Yellow onion            | Finely diced                                                                                                                   |
| 3 medium        | Carrots                 | Diced; peeled or not                                                                                                           |
| 6               | Garlic cloves           | Minced                                                                                                                         |
| 960ml (4 cups)  | Low-sodium vegetable broth | More as needed; use 1.2L (5 cups) for stovetop variant                                                                    |
| 450g (1 lb)     | Baby potatoes           | Quartered, peel on; or larger yellow/golden potatoes cut small                                                                 |
| 300g (1.5 cups) | French green lentils    | Also called Puy lentils; hold shape well and don't go mushy — preferred over regular green or brown lentils                   |
| 800g (1 can)    | Whole peeled tomatoes   | Crushed by hand, juices included; whole peeled preferred over pre-crushed for purer tomato flavour                            |
| 1.5 tsp         | Kosher salt             | Plus more to taste                                                                                                             |
|                 | Black pepper            | Freshly cracked, to taste                                                                                                      |
| 1/2 tsp         | Dijon mustard           | Added at the end                                                                                                               |
| 2 tsp           | Aged balsamic vinegar   | Use good quality — adds complex sweet tanginess; see note below for substitutes                                                |
|                 | **Bouquet garni:**      | 2 bay leaves + a few sprigs each of flat-leaf parsley, rosemary, sage, and thyme, tied with kitchen twine; 2-3 herbs is fine  |

## Instructions
**Notes:**
- French green lentils are strongly preferred — they hold their shape, don't go mushy, and have a subtle peppery flavour. Substitute black beluga lentils, or green/brown lentils (reduce pressure cook time to 10 min.).
- Use the best balsamic vinegar you have — it adds a complex sweet tanginess that makes the lentils taste meatier and the tomatoes sweeter. Standard cheap balsamic is just sour and doesn't help. No good balsamic? Use 1 tsp red wine or sherry vinegar instead, and add a pinch of sugar or maple syrup if too tangy.
- The soup thickens as it rests; reheat on the stove with splashes of broth. Stores 5-6 days in the fridge; freezes well for 4 months.

### Instant Pot
1. Select the Sauté setting. Once hot, add olive oil (2 tbsp), then onion with a pinch of salt. Cook for 6-8 min., stirring occasionally, until nicely golden and soft. Add a splash of water if the onion starts to brown too fast.
2. Add carrots (3) and garlic (6 cloves) with another pinch of salt. Cook for ~3 min. until the carrots just start to soften.
3. Pour in a splash of the broth to deglaze, scraping up any browned bits. Add the remaining broth (960ml), lentils (300g), potatoes (450g), bouquet garni, salt (1.5 tsp), and pepper. Stir gently.
4. Pour the hand-crushed tomatoes (800g) and their juices on top — do not stir. Keeping the tomatoes on top prevents them from sinking and triggering a Burn notice; pour the can as low and close to the surface as possible.
5. Seal the lid, set the valve to Sealing, and pressure cook on High for 12 min. Allow a natural pressure release for 10 min., then manually release remaining steam.
6. Remove the bouquet garni. Stir in Dijon mustard (1/2 tsp) and balsamic vinegar (2 tsp). Taste and adjust salt and pepper. Add more broth for a thinner consistency.

---

### Stovetop Variant
Follow steps 1-3 as above using a Dutch oven or large soup pot, using 1.2L (5 cups) broth. Add the tomatoes and stir everything together. Bring to a boil, then partially cover and simmer on low for 40-50 min. until lentils are tender. Finish as in step 6.
