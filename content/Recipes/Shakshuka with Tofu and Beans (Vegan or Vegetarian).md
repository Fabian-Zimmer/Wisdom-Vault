## Ingredients

## Instructions
**Note:** Vegan as is — add crumbled feta at the end to make it vegetarian.

1. Prepare the tofu: press and drain out as much water as possible until you have 400-450g. Crumble with your fingers into a bowl (cottage cheese-like texture) and mix in nutritional yeast flakes (3 tbsp), salt (1 tsp), and turmeric (1 tsp). Set aside.
2. In a wok with olive oil, sauté the onion for ~5 min. Add garlic and green chillies and cook for ~2 min. Add bell pepper and cook for a further 2-3 min.
3. Add cumin seeds (2 tsp), red chilli powder (3 tsp), and cayenne pepper (1/4 tsp). Stir constantly until the vegetables are well coated.
4. Add whole-tomato content (2 cans), tomato paste (3 tbsp), sugar (1/2 tbsp), salt (1 tsp), black pepper (1/2 tsp), and bay leaves (2). Mash the whole-tomatoes until dissolved into pureé-like consistency (can have small chunks). Stir well and simmer on low heat for ~30 min., stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened.
5. Taste the sauce and adjust salt, sugar, pepper, and spice as needed.
6. Add the tofu crumble and cannellini beans (400-500g, drained and rinsed). Simmer for ~10 min.
7. Top with chopped coriander leaves (handful), chopped mint leaves (small amount), and pitted and chopped black olives. 
	- If serving as vegetarian, finish with hand-crumbled feta.
