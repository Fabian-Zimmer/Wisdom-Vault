## Ingredients

### Cashew Cream (makes ~2 cups, used in both marinade and sauce)
| Amount     | Name          | Prep                                              |
| ---------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 3/4 cup    | Raw cashews   | Soaked, then blended with 1.5 cups water until smooth; or use raw sunflower seeds |

### Jackfruit Marinade
| Amount     | Name              | Prep                        |
| ---------- | ----------------- | --------------------------- |
| 3 cans     | Jackfruit         | Soaked overnight or boiled 10 min., drained and rinsed well |
| 1 cup      | Cashew cream      | See above                   |
| 1 tbsp     | Tandoori masala   |                             |
| 2 tbsp     | Fresh ginger      | Grated or minced            |
| 4          | Garlic cloves     | Minced                      |
| 3 tbsp     | Lemon juice       |                             |
| *Optional* | Salt              | 1 tsp or to taste           |

### Tikka Masala Sauce
| Amount     | Name                   | Prep                                    |
| ---------- | ---------------------- | --------------------------------------- |
| 1/3 cup    | Water                  |                                         |
| 1/4 cup    | Dried fenugreek leaves | Kasoori methi                           |
| 2 tbsp     | Tandoori masala        |                                         |
| 3 cups     | Tomato purée           |                                         |
| 1 cup      | Cashew cream           | See above                               |
| 2 tbsp     | Sugar                  | Ideally date sugar, or jaggery, or cane |
| 1/8 tsp    | Cayenne pepper         | Or to taste                             |
| *Optional* | Salt                   | To taste                                |

### To Serve
| Amount     | Name                    | Prep                        |
| ---------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------- |
|            | Fresh coriander leaves  | To garnish                  |
|            | Rice, quinoa, or bread  | For serving                 |

## Instructions
**Note:** The jackfruit needs to marinate for at least 3 hours — ideally overnight in the fridge. Plan ahead. The cashew cream is used in both the marinade and the sauce, so make the full batch at once in the food processor (small bowl and knife blade).

1. Drain the jackfruit and simmer in a pot of water for 10 min. Drain and rinse thoroughly to remove bitterness and excess sodium.
2. Make the cashew cream: blend soaked cashews (3/4 cup) with water (1.5 cups) in the food processor until completely smooth.
3. In a large bowl, combine cashew cream (1 cup), tandoori masala (1 tbsp), grated ginger (2 tbsp), minced garlic (4 cloves), lemon juice (3 tbsp), and salt if using. Add the jackfruit and mix to coat well. Cover and marinate in the fridge for at least 3h, ideally overnight.
4. Preheat oven to 175°C. Spread the marinated jackfruit evenly on a parchment-lined baking tray. Bake for 15 min., then broil (that's when top heating element is on in oven) for 3-5 min. until slightly charred.
5. Meanwhile, make the sauce: heat water (1/3 cup) in a large pan/pot. Add fenugreek leaves (1/4 cup) and tandoori masala (2 tbsp) and simmer for 1-2 min. to release the flavours.
6. Add tomato purée (3 cups), cashew cream (1 cup), sugar (2 tbsp, see ingredients for options), and cayenne pepper (1/8 tsp). Simmer for 5-10 min. Add extra water if the sauce is too thick. Adjust salt to taste.
7. Add the baked jackfruit to the sauce, stir to combine, and simmer together for a further 5 min.
8. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve with rice, quinoa, or rotis.
