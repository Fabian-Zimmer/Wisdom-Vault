## Ingredients

| 1 tbsp        | Black or brown mustard seeds | Or roughly crushed coriander seeds |
| 1 tbsp        | Cumin seeds           |                                         |

## Instructions
**Notes:** 
- The batter needs to rest for at least 1h — a slightly funky smell is normal. 
- Use an 18×13 inch rimmed oven sheet pan.

1. Whisk chickpea flour (235g) with lukewarm water (480ml) in stages to avoid lumps. Season with salt (1.75 tsp) and pepper (~1/2 tsp). Cover and rest at room temperature for 1-4h.
2. Preheat oven to 230°C. Line a rimmed sheet pan with parchment paper.
3. Make the tadka: heat olive oil (75g) in a small saucepan over medium-low until ~160°C. Add mustard seeds and swirl until they begin to pop, then add cumin seeds and swirl for 10-15 seconds until darkened. Pour immediately through a fine-mesh sieve set over a bowl, reserving both the spiced oil and the seeds separately.
4. Pour the infused oil onto the lined sheet pan and spread up the sides with a brush or spatula.
5. Whisk the batter again, pour evenly into the pan, and stir in the oil at the edges with a spoon. Scatter the reserved spices over the top and stir in to distribute.
6. Bake for 12 min., rotate the oven sheet pan 180°, then bake for a further ~10 min. until the edges are golden and the centre is set.
7. Meanwhile, make the yogurt sauce: stir together yogurt (140g), lime juice (2 tsp), grated/minced garlic (1 clove), salt, and pepper. Adjust to taste and refrigerate until serving.
8. Make the tomato salad: combine diced tomatoes (340g), sliced onion (1/4), chopped coriander, serrano or green chilli (1/2) if using, chaat masala (1/2 tsp), lime juice (2 tsp), salt, and pepper. Taste and adjust.
9. Cool the flatbread (ideally on a wire rack) for 5-10 min., then transfer to a cutting board and slice into squares. Serve with yogurt sauce spread on each square and tomato salad spooned on top.
