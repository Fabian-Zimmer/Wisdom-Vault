## Instructions
**Note:** Original recipe had 1/2 cup (~150-160g) maple syrup, which I replaced with 100g dates + 50g plant-based milk. Perhaps better to replace with date syrup, still reduced, maybe ~1/4 cup.

1. Make the flax egg: mix flax meal (1 tbsp) with warm water (3 tbsp) and leave to thicken for ~5 min. Meanwhile, soak dates (100g) in warm water for ~10 min., then drain.
2. Add the flax egg, black beans, almond butter (1/2 cup), almond flour (1/4 cup), cocoa powder (1/4 cup), soaked pitted dates (100g before soaking), plant-based milk (50g), vanilla extract (1.5 tsp), baking powder (1/4 tsp), baking soda (1/4 tsp), and espresso powder if using (1/4 tsp) to a blender. Blend until completely smooth.
3. Fold in chopped chocolate (~60g) and chopped walnuts (~60g).
4. Pour into a neutral-oil-lined baking form (rectangular is nice for making square pieces, round for cake pieces). Smooth the top with a spatula.
5. **To try:** Brush the top evenly with vegan egg wash (1 tbsp plant-based milk + 1 tbsp maple syrup). Hopefully this will make the top crispier.
6. Bake at 175°C for ~25 min in either a square or round baking form.
7. Leave to cool completely, ideally on wire-rack, for ~1h before slicing.
