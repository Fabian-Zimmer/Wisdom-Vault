## Ingredients
| Amount            | Name                  | Prep                                                       |
| ----------------- | --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 200g (1 cup)      | Long-grain white rice |                                                            |
| 15ml (1 tbsp)     | Neutral oil           | For cooking                                                |
| ~325g             | Firm tofu             | Pressed, fried in slabs then cooled and cut in cubes       |
| 75g (1/4 cup)     | Gochujang             | Or less for less heat                                      |
| 3-4               | Garlic cloves         | Minced                                                     |
| 45ml (3 tbsp)     | Light soy sauce       |                                                            |
| 7g (1/2 tbsp)     | Sugar                 | E.g., jaggery, cane, or coconut                            |
| 2 tbsp            | Flaxseed powder       | For sauce                                                  |
| 1 tbsp            | Tahini                | For sauce                                                  |
| 500g              | Cherry tomatoes       | Halved or quartered                                        |
| 1/2 cup           | Edamame               | Thawed                                                     |
| 20g               | Pumpkin seeds         | Toasted                                                    |
| 10g               | Sesame seeds          | Toasted                                                    |
| 2-3 sticks        | Spring onions         | White parts finely chopped; green parts sliced for garnish |
| *few tablespoons* | *Coconut milk*        | *Optional: for lightening sauce, if available*             |

## Instructions
1. Cook rice (200g) according to package instructions.
2. Meanwhile, heat oil (15ml) in a pan over medium-high heat and cook the tofu (~325g) in slabs until golden brown on both sides, ~10 min. Transfer to a bowl and set aside to cool, then cut into cubes.
3. While the tofu is cooking, mix together gochujang (75g), minced garlic (3-4 cloves), light soy sauce (45ml), sugar (1/2 tbsp, e.g., jaggery, cane, or coconut), flaxseed powder (2 tbsp), and tahini (1 tbsp) in a bowl. Set aside.
4. Add the finely chopped white parts of the spring onions to the now-empty tofu pan and sauté for a couple of minutes. Then with them toast pumpkin seeds (20g) and sesame seeds (10g) for a while. 
5. Add cherry tomatoes (500g) and edamame (1/2 cup) and cook for ~5-10 min.
6. Add the sauce and simmer for a further ~5 min. Stir in the tofu cubes towards the end and heat through.
7. *Optional: add coconut milk (a few tablespoons) to lighten sauce.*
8. Serve alongside the warm rice, topped with the toasted pumpkin and sesame seeds and the sliced green spring onion parts.
