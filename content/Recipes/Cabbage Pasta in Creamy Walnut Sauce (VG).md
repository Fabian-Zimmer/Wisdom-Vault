## Ingredients

## Instructions
1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Meanwhile, shred the cabbage using the food processor (large bowl and coarse shredding disk) to get ~10 cups.
2. In the food processor (small bowl, knife blade), pulse the walnuts (85g) to a coarse powder. Set aside.
3. Heat a large sauté pan over medium-high with oil (3 tbsp). Add the cabbage, salt (1 tsp), and plenty of black pepper. Toss to coat and cook for ~10 min., stirring occasionally, until tender, silky, and beginning to brown. If browning too quickly, deglaze with a splash of water and lower the heat.
4. Add garlic (4 cloves), thyme (1 tbsp), and red pepper flakes (1/2 tsp). Stir frequently for 2 min. until aromatic and garlic is just turning colour. Remove from heat and set aside.
5. Slightly salt the boiling water and cook the pasta until barely al dente (minimum package time). Scoop out a ladle of pasta water to save for later, then drain and return pasta to its pot.
6. While the pasta cooks, whisk together the creamy plant milk (300ml), nutritional yeast (1/2 cup), salt (1/2 tsp), and olive oil (2 tbsp) in a bowl.
7. Add the sauce and crushed walnuts (85g) to the pasta pot. Toss over medium-high heat for 1-2 min. until the pasta is well-coated and al dente, adding splashes of the saved pasta water as needed. Fold in the cabbage.
8. Remove from heat, stir in lemon juice (1 tbsp), and finish with generous black pepper. Serve topped with basil and a pinch of flaky sea salt if available.
