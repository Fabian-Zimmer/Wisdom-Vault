## Ingredients
| Amount     | Name                      | Prep                              |
| ---------- | ------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| 60g        | Honey                     | Or vegan alternative, e.g., agave |
| 30g        | Coconut oil               |                                   |
| 200g       | Rolled oats               |                                   |
| 80g        | Nuts of choice            | Roughly chopped to desire         |
| 20g        | Sesame seeds              | Toasted, white and/or black       |
| 30g        | Pumpkin seeds             |                                   |
| 20g        | Neutral oil               |                                   |
| 100g       | Vegan chocolate of choice | Chopped into small pieces         |
| 60g        | Raisins                   |                                   |
| 30g        | Buckwheat or other flour  | Optional                          |
| *Optional* | Cocoa powder              | ~20g                              |
|            | Pinch of salt             |                                   |

## Instructions
**Note:** Potentially vegan depending on honey — swap for agave or maple syrup to make fully vegan. If bars don't hold together after baking, increase honey (60g) and/or coconut oil (30g) next time — the original recipe also had 50g cane sugar which has been removed. A square baking form makes cutting into neat rectangular bars easier.

1. Melt honey (60g) and coconut oil (30g) together in a small pot until combined. Set aside.
2. In a large bowl, mix rolled oats (200g), roughly chopped nuts (80g), toasted sesame seeds (20g), pumpkin seeds (30g), neutral oil (20g), chopped chocolate (100g), raisins (60g), flour if using (30g), cocoa powder if using (~20g), and a pinch of salt.
3. Pour the melted honey and coconut oil over the dry mix and mix well until everything is evenly coated.
4. Transfer to a square baking form and pat down firmly and evenly.
5. Bake at 160°C for 30 min.
6. Let cool completely for ~1h before cutting — it will firm up as it cools and cutting too early will destroy the bars.
