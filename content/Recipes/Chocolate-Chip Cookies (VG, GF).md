## Ingredients
| Amount           | Name                            | Prep                                        |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| 235-265g (1 can) | Cooked chickpeas or white beans | Drained and rinsed                          |
| 90-100g          | Dates                           | Soaked in warm water ~10 min., then drained |
| 40-45g           | Plant-based milk                |                                             |
| 1 tsp            | Vanilla extract                 |                                             |
| 1/3 cup          | Almond butter (unsalted)        | Or a mix of almond + vegan butter           |
| 40-45g           | Oat flour                       |                                             |
| 1/4 tsp          | Ceylon cinnamon                 | Optional                                    |
| 1/2 tsp          | Baking soda                     |                                             |
| 1/2 tsp          | Baking powder                   |                                             |
| 1/4 tsp          | Salt                            |                                             |
| ~60g             | Vegan chocolate(s) of choice    | Chopped into small pieces                   |
| ~4 tbsp          | Cocoa powder                    | If making double chocolate version          |

## Instructions
**Notes:**
- Blending with little liquid can be stubborn — be patient and scrape down the sides as needed.
- Each dough piece will rise and spread into a medium-sized cookie.
- Scale ingredients towards the higher end if your can is closer to 265g.

1. Soak dates (90-100g) in warm water for ~10 min., then drain.
2. Add chickpeas or white beans (235-265g), soaked dates (90-100g), plant-based milk (40-45g), vanilla extract (1 tsp), almond butter (1/3 cup), oat flour (40-45g), cinnamon if using (1/4 tsp), baking soda (1/2 tsp), baking powder (1/2 tsp), and salt (1/4 tsp) to a food processor (large bowl and knife blade).
	- If making double chocolate version, also add cocoa powder (~4 tbsp).
3. Process until completely smooth, scraping down the sides as needed.
4. Transfer to a bowl and fold in chopped chocolate (~60g) by hand.
5. Preheat oven to 175°C. Line a baking tray. Scoop ~2-3 tbsp of dough per cookie, place on the tray, and flatten slightly with the back of a spoon.
6. Bake for 25-30 min. until golden. Leave to cool on the tray — they will firm up as they cool.
