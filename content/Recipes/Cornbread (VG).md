## Recommended Equipment
- Cast iron skillet (~24-26cm ideal) — essential for the crispy crust; or a 22x22cm metal baking pan (see notes)
- Oven mitts — the skillet gets very hot

## Ingredients

### Vegan Buttermilk
| Amount           | Name                | Prep                         |
| ---------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 360ml (1.5 cups) | Full-fat oat milk   | Soy or cashew milk also work |
| 2 tsp            | Apple cider vinegar |                              |

### Cornbread filling
| Amount               | Name                   | Prep                                                                                           |
| -------------------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                      | **Dry Ingredients:**   |                                                                                                |

## Instructions
**Notes:**
- Ideally use stone-ground cornmeal — standard boxed cornmeal is industrially processed and produces inferior flavour and texture.
- The 2:1 cornmeal to flour ratio is intentional — more flour makes it taste like corn cake, not cornbread.
- Do not skip the batter rest — it hydrates the cornmeal for a more moist and tender crumb.
- Do not overmix — lumps in the batter are fine and expected.
- Pour the batter slowly into the skillet or the hot butter will pool up to the surface.
- Stores up to 3 days in an airtight container. To reheat: oven at 190°C for 10-15 min. unwrapped (restores crispiness) or wrapped in foil (softer and moister).

2. Gently heat the full-fat plant milk (360ml, ~1.5 cups) in a small pot, then add apple cider vinegar (2 tsp) to curdle milk into vegan buttermilk, keep it warm for ~5-10min.
3. Preheat oven to 200°C with a rack in the middle (do this later if you're oven heats up very quickly).
4. In a large bowl, whisk together cornmeal (195g), flour (94g), baking powder (1 tbsp + 1 tsp), and salt (1/2 tsp). Make a well in the centre.
5. Pour the buttermilk, melted vegan butter (56g), olive oil (56g), brown sugar (40g), and agave (84g) into the well. Gently mix with a whisk until just combined — do not overmix, lumps are fine.
6. Fold in the finely chopped rosemary (1 heaping tbsp) with a spatula. Leave the batter to rest for 10 min. (or up to 1h) while the skillet heats up.
7. Place the cast iron skillet in the oven to heat for 10 min. Remove carefully with oven mitts and add vegan butter (2 tbsp) — it will melt almost immediately. Dust lightly with cornmeal (1 tsp). This combination will enhance flavor and prevent cornbread sticking to pan.
8. Slowly pour the batter into the hot skillet. Bake for 25-28 min. until the top is golden brown and a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean.
9. Transfer the pan to a wire rack and cool for 15-20 min. before slicing. Serve warm (can also serve with vegan butter, maple butter, or jam).

---

### Gluten-Free Variant
Replace the flour with the same amount of GF flour (ideally with xanthan gum, e.g. King Arthur measure-for-measure). Add an extra 60ml oat milk (total 420ml). Rest the baked cornbread for 30 min. before slicing instead of 15-20 min. — it will be slightly more moist and crumbly than the original.

### No Cast Iron Skillet
Use a 22×22cm metal baking pan. Add only 1 tbsp butter (instead of 2) to grease the pan. Add ~5 min. extra bake time to get nicely golden. The crust won't be as crispy.
