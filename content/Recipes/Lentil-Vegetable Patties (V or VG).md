## Instructions
**Note:** Soak the lentils 2h before starting. Vegan option: swap ghee for neutral oil and use plant-based yoghurt for the sauce.

1. Soak red lentils (200g) in water for 2h, then drain. Blend in the food processor (big bowl and knife) until smooth. Transfer to a large mixing bowl.
2. In a pan with ghee (or vegan butter or neutral oil if vegan), fry onion until softened, 2-3 min. Add garlic and cook for another minute. Add to the lentil mixture.
3. Using the food processor (medium bowl, coarse shredding disk), shred the carrot and potatoes. Add to the pan and fry over medium heat for ~3 min., then add to the lentil mixture.
4. Add oat bran (30g), chopped parsley, cumin (1/2 tsp), chilli flakes (1/2 tsp), salt, and a generous grind of black pepper to the bowl. Mix well until everything is combined.
5. Wet your hands (to prevent sticking) and form the mixture into patties. Place on a parchment-lined baking tray and brush with olive oil.
6. Bake at 180°C for 20 min. until golden.
7. Meanwhile, make the sauce: combine natural (or plant-based if vegan) yoghurt (100g), finely chopped pickles (80g), chopped dill, mustard (1 tsp), and salt to taste. Mix well and refrigerate until serving.
8. Serve patties with the sauce on the side.
