## Instructions

1. Cut peeled russet potatoes (600g) into halfs (or less) for medium-sized pieces. Pressure cook for 1 whistle on high heat (~8 on induction). The potatoes shouldn't be completely tender but should have a slightly starchy dry consistency.
	- Drain the potatoes and let them cool, either before or after crushing.
2. Meanwhile make your choice of sauce:
	1. Walnut-sage: blend together walnuts (50g), ricotta cheese (100-125g), plant-based cream (250ml), garlic clove (1), agave (1 tsp), cinnamon (1/2 tsp), nutmeg (1/2 tsp), finely cut sage leaves (~10), and dashes of salt and pepper to taste.
	2. Tomato-Carrot: Sautée red onion (1 big), garlic cloves (3-4), carrot (1 large) in a pot in olive oil for a few minutes. Add whole-tomato cans (2 x 400ml), basil leaves (~15g, handful), flaxseed powder if available (2 tbsp), aceto balsamico (a dash), and sugar (1/2 tsp), then simmer for a few minutes. Turn into desired smoothness with immersion blender.
3. Crush the potatoes into a paste with a fork or masher with holes. 
4. Spread some semolina flour on a large cutting/working board, add mashed potatoes, and add lots of the semolina flour on top, also add salt (1 tsp), and optional ground rosemary and thyme. 
5. Knead into a large ball, adding rest of or more flour as necessary. The dough can be slightly gooey/sticky inside but not on the outside.
6. Cut the dough into 4 equal pieces. 1 piece is one large-ish portion (for 1-2 people, depending).
7. Roll 1 piece into long thick strips (work in two or more batches depending on board size, usually 2 batches necessary to get thin enough strips), then cut into (very) small equal pieces (if too thick, they won't cook properly and it's not tasty).
8. Cook the gnocchi in boiling water (optionally salted) until they float to the top, then let cook for approximately 1 minute while floating. Remove with a sieve or slotted spoon/ladle.
9. Heat up appropriate amount of sauce in wok/pan, add 1 portion cooked gnocchi, and top with walnuts (roughly chopped) and parmesan (finely grated).
