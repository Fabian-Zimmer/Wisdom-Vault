## Banana Pancakes
https://peacefulcuisine.com/2015/09/banana-pancake/
### Ingredients
| Amount | Name           | Prep                      |
| ------ | -------------- | ------------------------- |
| 100g   | Pastry flour   | Or all-purpose flour      |
| 10g    | Almonds        |                           |
| 10g    | Walnuts        |                           |
| 10g    | Coconut flakes |                           |
| 10g    | Sugar          | E.g., cane or coconut     |
| 120g   | Soy milk       |                           |
| 50g    | Banana         | Mashed while mixing later |
| 1 tsp  | Baking powder  |                           |

### Instructions
1. In the food processor (small bowl, knife blade), pulse the almonds (10g), walnuts (10g), and coconut flakes (10g) a few times until a fine-ish powder forms. Transfer to a mixing bowl.
2. Add flour (100g), sugar (10g), baking powder (1 tsp), soy milk (120g), and banana (50g). Mash the banana into the mixture by hand, then stir everything together until just combined — don't overmix.
3. Cook in a lightly oiled pan over medium heat, flipping once bubbles form on the surface.
