## Instructions

### Cashew Cream
- Blend cashews (1.5 cups) and water (1.5 cups) until smooth. Set aside without washing the blender.

### Roasted Tomato Soup
1. Preheat oven to 200°C on air convection. 
2. Spread garlic (1 bulb) that was crushed and peeled, quartered red onion (1), red bell pepper (1), big tomatoes (~7, quartered), and cherry tomatoes (900g) on a large baking tray. Drizzle with avocado oil (2 tbsp), season with a generous pinch of salt and pepper, add thyme and rosemary and toss to coat, burying the herbs under the vegetables. Roast for 60-70 min.
3. About 10 min. before the vegetables are done, sauté the diced red onion (1) in chilli oil (3 tbsp) in a big stock pot (that can fit all the ~1.8L of soup) over medium heat for a few minutes. Add tomato paste (1 tbsp), sweet paprika or red chilli powder (1 tbsp), salt (2 tsp), maple syrup (1/ tbsp), and balsamic glaze (1 tbsp). Stir and cook for 1 min. Add beetroot without cutting it (80g, ~1/2 medium-big) and stir.
4. When the vegetables are done, set a few aside for garnish. Strip the herb leaves from their branches and discard the branches. Add all roasted vegetables, herb leaves, fresh basil (handful, reserving some for garnish), and vegetable stock (480ml) to the pot. Bring to a boil.
5. Carefully transfer everything to the blender and blend until completely smooth. Return to the pot and stir in cashew cream (1/3 cup).
6. Serve topped with reserved roasted vegetables, fresh basil, cracked pepper, and a drizzle of cashew cream.
