## Ingredients

### Salad
| Amount            | Name                          | Prep                                                            |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 200g              | Dried vermicelli rice noodles |                                                                 |
| 300g              | Green/French beans            | Trimmed, cut in half widthways                                  |
| ~1/2 large (200g) | Cucumber                      | Quartered lengthways, deseeded, cut into 2mm slices at an angle |
| ~2 (20g)          | Red chillies                  | Deseeded and julienned                                          |
| ~1/2 (60g)        | Red onion                     | Peeled and thinly sliced                                        |
| 1/4 tsp           | Salt                          |                                                                 |

### Tamarind Dressing
| Amount  | Name              | Prep                                           |
| ------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 70ml    | Tamarind paste    | Shop-bought; or double if extracting from pulp |
| 60ml    | Maple syrup       |                                                |
| ~4 tbsp | (Light) soy sauce |                                                |
| 75ml    | Lime juice        | From 3-4 green limes                           |
| 55ml    | Groundnut oil     | A.k.a. peanut oil                              |

### Mushroom Laab
| Amount | Name                | Prep                                                                               |
| ------ | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 25g    | Basmati rice        | Toasted and ground to fine powder                                                  |
| 300g   | Chestnut mushrooms  | Finely chopped; use food processor knife blade (see instructions)                  |
| 300g   | Oyster mushrooms    | Roughly torn                                                                       |
| 45ml   | Groundnut oil       | A.k.a. peanut oil                                                                  |
| 1 tsp  | Red chilli flakes   |                                                                                    |
| 120g   | Raw peanuts         | Lightly roasted and finely chopped (pulse in small bowl + knife of food processor) |
| 5g     | Mint leaves         |                                                                                    |
| 5g     | (Thai) basil leaves |                                                                                    |
| 10g    | Coriander leaves    |                                                                                    |

## Instructions
**Note:** The noodles and laab can be made up to 3 hours ahead — don't add the herbs or assemble until ready to serve.

1. Toast basmati rice (25g) in a small dry pan over medium heat for ~10 min. until golden and nutty-smelling. Leave to cool, then grind to a fine powder in the food processor (small bowl and knife blade) or a spice grinder. Set aside.
2. Put dried vermicelli rice noodles (200g) in a heatproof bowl, cover with 1 litre of boiling water, and leave to soften for 10 min. Drain, run under cold water, and drain again.
3. Meanwhile, bring a small pot of lightly salted water to the boil. Add beans (300g) and boil for ~3-5 min. Drain and run under cold water to stop cooking, then drain again.
4. In a large bowl, toss the noodles with the beans, cucumber (~1/2 large, 200g), chillies (~2, 20g), red onion (~1/2, 60g), and 1/4 tsp salt. Set aside.
5. Make the dressing: whisk together tamarind paste (70ml), maple syrup (60ml), soy sauce (2 tbsp), lime juice (~3 tbsp), and groundnut oil (55ml). Set aside.
6. Heat groundnut oil (45ml) in a large sauté pan over high heat. Add the chestnut and oyster mushrooms (600g total) and cook for ~12-15 min., stirring occasionally, until all liquid has cooked away and the mushrooms are browned. Add chilli flakes (1 tsp) and cook for 2 min. more. Off the heat, stir through the peanuts (120g), ground rice, remaining soy sauce (2 tbsp), and the rest of the lime juice (~2 tbsp). Keep everything warm.
7. When ready to serve, add the herbs (mint, Thai basil, coriander) to the noodle salad along with half the tamarind dressing. Toss to combine.
8. Pour the remaining dressing over the mushroom laab and mix well. Spread the laab out on a platter, then top with the noodles so the laab can still be seen around the edges.
