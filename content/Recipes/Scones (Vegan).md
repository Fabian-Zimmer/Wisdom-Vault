## Instructions
**Note:** The dough will be wet — this is normal. Work with wet hands when shaping.

Prep: Preheat oven to 220°C (do this later if your oven quickly preheats). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.

1. In a cup or blender, combine plant milk (1 cup), cashew butter (3 tbsp), apple cider vinegar (2 tbsp), and vanilla extract (1 tsp). Mix or blend until smooth.
2. In a large bowl, combine almond flour (2 cups), oat flour (1 cup), date sugar (3/4 cup), arrowroot powder (6 tbsp), baking powder (4.5 tsp), vanilla powder (1/2 tsp), and cinnamon and salt if using. Add chocolate chips or berries and stir through.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and mix just until combined.
4. Turn the dough out onto a sheet of parchment paper. With wet hands, shape into a disc ~3cm thick. Cut into 6 equal triangular pieces and transfer to the lined baking sheet; Or choose any form of your liking, thick-small disks, half-domes, etc.
5. Bake for 12-14 min. Cool on the tray for 5-10 min. before handling.
6. If making the icing, blend soaked cashews (1/2 cup), plant milk (1/2 cup), and vanilla extract (1/2 tsp) until completely smooth. Adjust plant milk for thinner or thicker consistency. Drizzle over cooled scones.
