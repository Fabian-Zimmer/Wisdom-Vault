## Recommended Equipment
- Large mixing bowl
- Clean work surface or silicone baking mat — for kneading and rolling
- Parchment paper
- Fine mesh sieve — for dusting powdered sugar
- Airtight container(s) — for chilling in the fridge

## Ingredients

### Raisin Soak
| Amount | Name         | Prep                                                                     |
| ------ | ------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 140g   | Raisins      | Can be a mix of raisins                                                  |
| 40g    | Orange juice | Best alcohol-free sub for rum; or apple juice or strong brewed black tea |

| 100g   | Soy milk           | Or other plant milk; slightly warm                                                        |

| 30g    | Neutral oil        | E.g., sunflower                                                                           |
| 20g    | Warm water         |                                                                                           |

| Amount | Name           | Prep                                                                    |
| ------ | -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 100g   | Mixed nuts     | E.g., macadamia, brazil, almonds, walnuts; cut into small-medium pieces |
|        | Coconut oil    | A little, melted, optional — mixed into brushing liquid                 |

## Instructions
**Notes:** 
- Stollen dough does not rise like regular bread — 1.5 times the original size is sufficient for the first rise. The second rise only needs to reach 1.2-1.5 times bigger. 
- Total time needed is ~4-5h including both rises and chilling.
- For traditional way, use rum instead of the recommended non-alcoholic substitutes.

### Prep
0. Soak raisins (140g) in orange juice (40g) — or your preferred non-alcoholic substitute. Leave to soak while you prepare the dough. Reserve the liquid after draining for brushing later.

### Dough
1. Mix warm water (20g) and dried yeast (5g) in a small bowl or cup and set aside for 10-15 min. until foamy.
2. Melt coconut oil (20g) if solid.
3. *Dry ingredients:* in a large bowl, combine the strong bread flour (200g), almond flour (60g), salt (2g), and cinnamon (2g) and mix together well. 
4. *Wet ingredients:* add soy milk (100g), melted coconut oil (20g), and neutral oil (30g). Add the yeast mixture last and mix until a dough forms.
5. Turn out onto a clean surface and knead by hand for 5-10 min. until the dough is smooth and soft. If too sticky, add a little flour; if too stiff, add a small splash of plant milk or water.
6. Form into a ball, place back in the bowl, cover with a damp towel or plate, and leave to rise in a warm place for 1-2h until ~1.5 times its original size.

### Filling
6. While the dough rises, cut the mixed nuts (100g) into small-medium pieces. Drain the soaked raisins, reserving the soaking liquid in a bowl.
7. Once the dough has risen, add the drained raisins and nuts directly to the dough ball. Knead by hand until fairly evenly distributed throughout. If the dough becomes sticky from the soaking liquid, add a little flour.

### Shape & Second Rise
8. On a non-stick surface (parchment paper on a cutting board or a silicone baking mat), roll the dough into a medium-large rectangle.
9. Roll up both long sides of the rectangle inward, so they overlap to ~90%, and so the loaf runs along the long axis and has maximum length.
10. Cover loosely and leave to rise for ~1h until 1.2-1.5 times bigger.

### Bake & Finish
11. Preheat oven to 180°C. Bake for 30 min.
12. Remove from the oven. While still hot, brush the surface liberally with the reserved soaking liquid, optionally mixed with a little melted coconut oil. Leave to cool until the surface is no longer hot to the touch.
13. Dust generously with powdered sugar through a fine mesh sieve until the loaf is covered in a thin, even layer.
14. Transfer to an airtight container (cut in half if needed and fit into 2 containers) and chill in the fridge until fully cold before slicing.
