## Recommended Equipment
- Dutch oven or large soup pot
- Blender — for making cashew-miso paste together with partially blending the soup (can also use immersion blender for the latter)
- Kitchen twine — for the bouquet garni

## Ingredients

### Mushroom Stock (optional but recommended)
| Amount         | Name                    | Prep                                                                             |
| -------------- | ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 28g (1 oz)     | Dried porcini mushrooms | Soaked in 360ml water for 30 min.; stock reserved, mushrooms washed and sliced   |

### Soup
| Amount          | Name                  | Prep                                                                                                                       |
| --------------- | --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 450g (16 oz)    | Fresh mushrooms       | Mixed varieties ideally — e.g. cremini, shiitake, oyster, maitake; slice firm ones, tear soft ones                         |
| 1 tbsp          | Olive oil             | Extra virgin                                                                                                               |
| 1 large         | Sweet onion           | Finely chopped                                                                                                             |
| 8               | Garlic cloves         | Finely chopped                                                                                                             |
| 2 tbsp          | Vegan butter          | Or more olive oil                                                                                                          |
| 120ml (1/2 cup) | Dry white wine        | E.g. Sauvignon Blanc, Pinot Grigio; avoid oaky wines like Chardonnay; or sub 60ml broth + 1 tbsp sherry/white wine vinegar |
| 720ml (3 cups)  | Vegetable broth       | Use 960ml (4 cups) if not making mushroom stock                                                                            |
| 1 large sprig   | Rosemary              | For bouquet garni; or sub sage                                                                                             |
| 2 sprigs        | Thyme                 | For bouquet garni; or sub sage                                                                                             |
| 2               | Bay leaves            | Dried, for bouquet garni                                                                                                   |
|                 | Salt and black pepper | To taste                                                                                                                   |

### For Blending
| Amount        | Name              | Prep                                                                                      |
| ------------- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 70g (1/2 cup) | Raw cashews       | No soaking needed with a high-speed blender; otherwise soak in boiling water for 10 min. |
| 1 tbsp        | White miso paste  | Or red miso for a more robust taste                                                       |
| 1 tbsp        | Soy sauce or tamari | Use tamari for gluten-free                                                              |
| 1/4 tsp       | Dijon mustard     | Optional; adds a pop of brightness                                                        |

### To Serve
| Amount | Name                        | Prep                    |

|        | Crusty or toasted bread     | For dipping             |

## Instructions
**Notes:**
- Using a mix of mushroom varieties adds depth of flavour and textural variation — shiitakes for meaty umami, oyster for delicacy, cremini as an affordable base.
- If no dried mushrooms, add 1/2 tsp porcini mushroom powder with the vegetable broth and use 960ml vegetable broth total.
- The soup thickens as it rests; thin with a splash of broth or water when reheating.
- Stores 5-6 days in the fridge; freezes well.

1. If making mushroom stock: place dried porcini mushrooms (28g) in a bowl, cover with room temperature water (360ml), and force them to be submerged by adding a smaller bowl on top to press them into the water. Soak for 30 min. until softened.
	1. Meanwhile, make the bouquet garni: line up rosemary (1 sprig), thyme (2 sprigs), and bay leaves (2) and tie together tightly with kitchen twine. Set aside.
	2. Also meanwhile, prep fresh mushrooms (450g): slice cremini and shiitake (remove shiitake stems); tear oyster and maitake into strips with your hands, discarding any tough ends.
	3. When done soaking, scoop out the porcini mushrooms, strain the stock through a fine mesh sieve, and reserve. Wash rehydrated mushrooms in fresh water, agitating to remove grit — repeat a few times. Slice and set aside.
2. Heat olive oil (1 tbsp) in a Dutch oven or large pot over medium-high. Add onion with a pinch of salt and cook for 4-5 min. until starting to soften. Add garlic (8 cloves) and cook for 1-2 min.
3. Add vegan butter (2 tbsp) and once melted, add all the fresh mushrooms and the rehydrated dried mushrooms if using. Cook for ~5 min. until softened. Season with salt and pepper at the end — not before, as early salt draws out water and prevents browning.
4. Pour in the wine (120ml) or broth + vinegar mix (60ml + 1 tbsp) and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom. Simmer for 3-4 min. until the alcohol smell has dissipated.
5. Slowly pour in the mushroom stock if using (reserving the last tablespoon, which is sediment-heavy), then the vegetable broth (720ml, or 960ml if not using mushroom stock). Add the bouquet garni and bring to a boil. Cover, reduce heat, and simmer for 15 min.
6. Remove the bouquet garni. Scoop out 1/3 to 1/2 of the total soup (~2-2.5 cups / 550-600g), making sure to get some mushrooms, and transfer to a blender. Add cashews (70g), miso (1 tbsp), and soy sauce (1 tbsp). Blend starting on low then increasing to high speed until completely smooth and creamy.
	- *Without stand blender:* transfer the specified amount of soup to a smaller pot, add cashews, miso, and soy sauce, then use an immersion blender directly in that pot until smooth. Merge back into the larger pot.
7. Stir the blended soup back into the pot. Simmer for 5-10 min. until the flavours have melded and the soup has thickened slightly. Taste and season with salt and pepper.
	- *Optional:* add Dijon mustard (1/4 tsp) for a slight brightness.
8. Ladle into bowls. Finish each with a drizzle of olive oil and a small handful of chopped parsley and/or chives. Serve with crusty or toasted bread.
