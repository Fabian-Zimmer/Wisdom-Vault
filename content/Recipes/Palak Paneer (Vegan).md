## Instructions
**Note:** Prepare the spinach first — finely chop 2 handfuls and set aside. Blanch the rest briefly in boiling water (in batches with slotted ladle/spoon to lift out spinach after a few minutes and put into container for blending), then blend to a purée and set aside separately.

1. In a big wok or pot, heat olive oil (3 tbsp) and ghee (2 tbsp, or 1 extra tbsp oil). Add bay leaves (4), cloves (4), and jeera seeds (2 tsp). Let sizzle briefly.
2. Add garlic (8 cloves), green chillies (4), and ginger. Let simmer for a couple of minutes.
3. Add red onions (4) and a pinch of asafoetida. Cook until the onions are soft and golden.
4. Add turmeric (2 tsp), coriander powder (2 tsp), black pepper (2 tsp), red chilli powder (2 tsp), and kasoori methi (1 tbsp). Stir well.
5. Add whole-tomatoes can (400g) and squash them until pureéd. Add salt to taste, a little water, then stir.
6. Add the finely chopped spinach and a good amount of water. Stir and cook for a few minutes.
7. Add the cooking cream (~200g) and the blanched, puréed spinach. Simmer for a few minutes.
8. Add a pinch of nutmeg, taste and adjust salt if necessary.
9. Add the paneer (1kg, softened in hot water) and simmer gently until heated through.
