## Ingredients

### Chai Concentrate (~60-70g)
| Amount  | Name             | Prep              |
| ------- | ---------------- | ----------------- |
| 1 tsp   | Cardamom pods    | Cracked in mortar |
| 1/2 tsp | Allspice berries | Cracked in mortar |
| 1/4 tsp | Black pepper     | Cracked in mortar |
| 1/4 tsp | Cloves           | Cracked in mortar |
| 1/2     | Cinnamon stick   | Cracked in mortar |
| 1/2     | Star anise       | Cracked in mortar |
| 15g     | Fresh ginger     | Julienned         |
| ~80ml   | Water            |                   |

### Dough
| Amount | Name                 | Prep                                                     |
| ------ | -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| 200g   | White rice           | Soaked a few hours, drained                              |
| 100g   | White quinoa         | Soaked a few hours, drained                              |
| 270g   | Water total          | Including absorbed water, espresso, and chai concentrate |
| 60g    | Espresso             |                                                          |
| 60g    | Chai concentrate     | See above                                                |
| 25g    | Coconut oil          |                                                          |
| 5g     | Dry yeast            |                                                          |
| 20g    | Sugar                | E.g., beet or cane                                       |
| 3g     | Salt                 |                                                          |
| 20g    | Psyllium husk powder |                                                          |

### Filling
| Amount        | Name               | Prep                              |
| ------------- | ------------------ | --------------------------------- |
| 200g          | Nut butter         | Microwaved to soften              |
| 60g           | Jaggery or brown sugar |                               |
| 4 tsp         | Cinnamon powder    |                                   |
|               | Vegan butter       | Melted, optional for more volume  |
|               | Water              | A little, if needed to loosen     |

## Instructions

**Note:** Start by soaking the rice and quinoa, as this takes a few hours. The additional 270g total water for the dough includes the absorbed water from soaking, the espresso, and the chai concentrate — measure the drained rice and quinoa + espresso + chai first to know how much plain water remains needed to reach 270g extra water.

### Chai Concentrate
1. Lightly crack all spices (cardamom, allspice, black pepper, cloves, cinnamon stick, star anise) in a mortar. Add ginger julienne slices.
2. Toast the cracked spices in a medium-sized dry pan/pot for a couple of minutes until fragrant.
3. Add water (~80ml), and simmer for ~10 min. (ideally until reduced to ~60-70g).
4. Strain through a metal sieve to remove all spices. Set aside to cool.

### Dough
5. Soak rice (200g) and quinoa (100g) together for a few hours. Drain and measure the amount of absorbed water — subtract this from 270g to know how much plain water to add later (see note above).
6. Prepare the espresso (60g) and measure out the chai concentrate (60g).
7. Add soaked rice, soaked quinoa, espresso, chai concentrate, remaining water, coconut oil (25g), yeast (5g), beet sugar (20g), and salt (3g) to a blender. Blend until smooth.
   (ideally something more powerful than a staff mixer, otherwise dough won't form well)
8. Transfer to a bowl, add psyllium husk powder (20g), and mix well. 
9. Leave to rise until doubled in size.

### Filling
10. Mix nut butter (200g, microwaved to soften), jaggery or brown sugar (60g), and cinnamon (4 tsp). Add a little water if needed to loosen, and more melted vegan butter if you want more filling volume.

### Assembly & Baking
11. Roll out the dough thinly on a well-floured surface into large rectangle. Spread the filling evenly across the whole surface.
12. Braide and shape the rolls (see Peaceful Cuisine's dirty chai cinnamon roll video for technique).
13. Leave to rise a second time until doubled in size.
14. Bake at 180°C for ~30 min. until golden.
