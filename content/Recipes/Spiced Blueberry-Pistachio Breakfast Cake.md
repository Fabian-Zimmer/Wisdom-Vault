## Recommended Equipment
- 9×5 inch loaf pan
- Parchment paper
- Wire rack

## Ingredients

### Wet
| Amount         | Name                   | Prep/Notes                                                          |
| -------------- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 240g (1 cup)   | Unsweetened applesauce |                                                                     |
| 6-8            | Dates                  | Originally maple syrup (80g, 1/4cup) + coconut sugar (70g, 1/2 cup) |
| 128g (1/2 cup) | Nut butter             | E.g., almond or cashew                                              |
| 240ml (1 cup)  | Plant-based milk       | E.g., almond or soy                                                 |
| 1 tsp          | Vanilla extract        |                                                                     |

### Dry
| Amount           | Name                    | Prep                                                                |
| ---------------- | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 219g (1.75 cups) | Whole-grain spelt flour | Plus 2 tbsp (16-18g) extra for tossing blueberries                  |
| 48g (1/2 cup)    | Rolled oats             | Old-fashioned/whole rolled oats                                     |
| 2 tbsp           | Flaxseed powder         |                                                                     |
| 2 tsp            | Ground cinnamon         | Cassia cinnamon; use at least 3 tsp if using milder Ceylon cinnamon |
| 1 tsp            | Ground ginger           |                                                                     |
| 1/2 tsp          | Ground nutmeg           | Freshly grated from whole nutmeg gives superior flavour             |
| 1/2 tsp          | Ground allspice         |                                                                     |
| 1.5 tsp          | Baking powder           |                                                                     |
| 1/4 tsp          | Baking soda             |                                                                     |
| 1/4 tsp          | Fine sea salt           |                                                                     |

### Fold-ins
| Amount                | Name               | Prep                                                                                                                   |
| --------------------- | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 227g (1.5 cups)       | Blueberries        | Fresh or defrosted frozen; if frozen, pat dry with paper towels to remove excess liquid before using                   |
| 50g (~heaped 1/3 cup) | Shelled pistachios | Finely chopped; optionally roast whole at 150°C for 10 min. (or 4-5 min. if already chopped) until golden and fragrant |
| 20g (~2 tbsp)         | Hemp seeds         |                                                                                                                        |

## Instructions
**Note:** The batter should be slightly thick, not pourable — adjust flour or milk accordingly. Tossing the blueberries in flour before folding in prevents them from sinking to the bottom. Removing the coconut sugar and maple syrup means the batter will be less sweet than the original — taste and adjust by adding an extra date or two if needed.

1. Preheat oven to 176°C. 
2. Line a 9×5 inch loaf pan with parchment paper, letting it extend a few inches over the sides for easy lifting.
3. Soak dates (6-8) in warm water for ~10 min. if not very soft, then drain.
4. *Food processor method (recommended):*
   Add all wet ingredients — applesauce (240g), dates (6-8), nut butter (128g), plant-based milk (240ml), and vanilla extract (1 tsp) — to the food processor (large bowl, knife blade). Then layer the dry ingredients on top — spelt flour (219g), oats (48g), flaxseed powder (2 tbsp), cinnamon (2 tsp), ginger (1 tsp), nutmeg (1/2 tsp), allspice (1/2 tsp), baking powder (1.5 tsp), baking soda (1/4 tsp), and salt (1/4 tsp) — and process until well combined and smooth.
   *Bowl method:* 
   Whisk the wet ingredients together in a medium bowl until smooth. In a large bowl combine the dry ingredients, then pour the wet into the dry and stir until incorporated.
5. In a small bowl, toss blueberries (227g) with the reserved spelt flour (2 tbsp / 16-18g). Using a rubber spatula, fold the blueberries, chopped pistachios (50g), and hemp seeds (20g) into the batter by hand. Pour into the prepared loaf pan.
6. Bake for 55-65 min. (even up to 70-80 min. is okay) until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean — the dates and applesauce make this loaf very moist, so it may need the extra time. Cool in the pan on a wire rack, then lift out using the parchment handles. Serve warm or at room temperature.
