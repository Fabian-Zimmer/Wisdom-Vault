## Ingredients
| Amount         | Name                     | Prep                                    |
| -------------- | ------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| 900g-1000g     | Baby/small waxy potatoes | Scrubbed, peel on, halved if large      |
| ~56g (1/4 cup) | Tahini                   |                                         |
| ~112g          | Yoghurt                  | Coconut for Vegan, Greek for Vegetarian |
| 2 tsp          | Dijon mustard            |                                         |
| 2 tsp          | Yellow mustard           |                                         |
| 1              | Garlic clove             | Minced                                  |
| 1/2            | Lemon                    | Zested, then juiced                     |
| 1 tsp          | Celery seeds             |                                         |
| 1 tbsp         | Caper brine              | Liquid from caper jar                   |
| 5-8g           | Fresh dill leaves        | Finely chopped                          |
| 1 tbsp         | Capers                   | Drained                                 |
| 1/4-1/2        | Onion                    | Thinly sliced into rings                |
| 1              | Jalapeño pepper          | Sliced                                  |
| 1-2 tbsp       | Fresh chives             | Chopped                                 |
|                | Salt                     | To taste                                |
|                | Herb salt                | Generous amount, to top                 |
|                | Black pepper             | Freshly cracked                         |

## Instructions
1. Wash and scrub the potatoes (900-1000g), leaving the peel on. Halve any larger ones. Cook in salted water (1/2 tsp salt) until just tender with a little resistance to a fork, ~10-15 min. Drain and leave to cool slightly.
2. Meanwhile, make the dressing in a large bowl: combine tahini (~56g), Coconut/Greek Yoghurt (~112g), Dijon mustard (2 tsp), yellow mustard (2 tsp), minced garlic (1 clove), lemon zest and juice (1/2 lemon), celery seeds (1 tsp), caper brine (1 tbsp), chopped fresh dill (5-8g), salt (1/2 tsp), and a generous crack of black pepper. Mix well.
3. Add the warm potatoes to the dressing and toss to coat. Add capers (1 tbsp), onion rings (1/4-1/2), jalapeño slices, and chives (1-2 tbsp). Toss gently.
4. Taste and adjust salt. Finish with a generous topping of herb salt before serving.
