## Ingredients
| Amount    | Name                  | Prep                               |
| --------- | --------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 355ml     | Cashew nut milk       | Home-made                          |
| 2 tsp     | Agar-agar powder      |                                    |
| 1/2 tsp   | Black salt            | For egg white                      |
| 1/2 cup   | Canned chickpeas      |                                    |
| 1/4 cup   | Chickpea water        | From canned chickpeas (aquafaba)   |
| 1/4 tsp   | Pink salt             | For egg yolk                       |
| 1/2 tsp   | Turmeric powder       |                                    |
| 2 tbsp    | Grapeseed oil         |                                    |

## Instructions
**Note:** You will need an egg-shaped freezer tray for this recipe. Make the cashew nut milk beforehand.

1. Egg white: Blend cashew nut milk (355ml), agar-agar powder (2 tsp), and black salt (1/2 tsp) together. Transfer to a pot and heat, stirring continuously, until it just comes to a boil. Immediately pour into the egg-shaped freezer tray and refrigerate for 40 min.
2. Egg yolk: Blend chickpeas (1/2 cup), chickpea water (1/4 cup), pink salt (1/4 tsp), and turmeric (1/2 tsp) until smooth. Pour into a bowl and stir in grapeseed oil (2 tbsp).
3. Once the egg whites have set, scoop a semi-sphere out of each and fill with the yolk mixture.
