## Ingredients
| Amount    | Name                   | Prep/Notes                         |
| --------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| 300g      | Rice flour             | Plus extra for dusting             |
| 30g       | Beet sugar             | Or brown cane sugar                |
| 15g       | Neutral oil            | E.g., sunflower oil                |
| 20g       | Psyllium husks         |                                    |
| 3g        | Dry yeast              |                                    |
| 3g        | Salt                   |                                    |
| 330g min. | Warm water             | Adjust as needed                   |
| 200g      | Nut butter             | For filling                        |
| 60g       | Jaggery or brown sugar | Dark fluffy brown sugar works best |
| 4 tsp     | Cinnamon powder        | For filling                        |
|           | Vegan butter           | Fully melted, for brushing         |

## Instructions
**Note:** Half the dough amount makes ~5 rolls. See Peaceful Cuisine's video (link in properties above) for the braiding technique. The dough may not fully bind — can happen with rice flour; it will still bake well.

1. Combine rice flour (300g), sugar (30g), oil (15g), psyllium husks (20g), yeast (3g), salt (3g), and warm water (330g min.) in a bowl. Mix until roughly combined, then leave to double in volume for 30-45 min. — an off oven works well for isolation and steady room temperature.
2. Knead the dough by hand on a flat surface for a long time, adding warm water gradually until the dough is gooey, sticky, and as stretchy as possible. Depending on the rice flour used, can take much more than 330g water, and dough will/can be much wetter than usual (Gluten-free baking is different, read online). Rest in the fridge for ~2h.
3. Mix the filling: nut butter (200g), jaggery or brown sugar (60g), and cinnamon (4 tsp). Add a little water if needed to loosen, and more melted vegan butter if you want more filling volume.
4. Dust the surface generously with rice flour and flatten the dough into a large, thin square. Spread the filling evenly across the whole surface.
5. Fold the top third down and the bottom third up to create a strip. Roll out again along short axis only.
6. For each roll, cut to desired width, then cut twice within each piece keeping 3 braids attached. Mini-braid and fold-and-roll from top to bottom (see Peaceful Cuisine's video).
7. Brush rolls with fully melted vegan butter. Bake at 180°C for ~30-40 min. (with brown rice flour it took ~40min.) until golden and cooked through.
