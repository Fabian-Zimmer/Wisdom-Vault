## Ingredients
| Amount    | Name                   | Prep/Notes                                     |
| --------- | ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| 300g      | Rice flour             | Plus extra for dusting                         |
| 30g       | Beet sugar             | Or brown cane sugar                            |
| 15g       | Olive oil              |                                                |
| 20g       | Psyllium husks         | Original was 10g                               |
| 3g        | Dry yeast              |                                                |
| 3g        | Salt                   |                                                |
| 330g min. | Warm water             | Adjust as needed                               |
| 200g      | Nut butter             | For filling, (microwave) warmed to soften      |
| 60g       | Jaggery or brown sugar | Some dark fluffy brown sugar works best        |
| 4 tsp     | Cinnamon powder        | For filling                                    |
| 1 tbsp    | Plant-based milk       | for brushing (e.g., soy)                       |
| 1/2 tbsp  | Sweetener              | for brushing (e.g., maple syrup or cane sugar) |

## Instructions
**Note:** Half the dough amount makes ~5 rolls. See Peaceful Cuisine's video (link in properties above) for the braiding technique. The dough may not fully bind — can happen with rice flour; it will still bake well.


### Making the dough
1. Combine rice flour (300g), sugar (30g), psyllium husks (20g), yeast (3g), and salt (3g) salt in a bowl and mix; ideally in a stand mixer, food processor can also work; by hand is tough but doable with patience and lots of work.
2. While machine is running, or while you're kneading: pour in olive oil (15g) and warm water bit by bit (~330g, adjust lower/higher to desired consistency). This can take up to ~10 minutes.
   Ideally dough should be sticky and as stretchy as possible. Depending on the rice flour used, can take more than 330g water, and such gluten-free dough can be much wetter than gluten dough.
3. Leave to double in volume for 30-45 min (a turned off oven works well for isolation and steady room temperature). 
   Then put in the fridge for a few hours (2-3h ideally).

### Filling and finishing
1. Mix the filling: nut butter (200g, microwave to soften), jaggery or brown sugar (60g), and cinnamon (4 tsp). Add a little water if needed to loosen, and more melted vegan butter if you want more filling volume.
2. Dust the surface generously with rice flour and flatten the dough into a large, thin square. Spread the filling evenly across the whole surface.
3. Fold the top third down and the bottom third up to create a strip. Roll out again along short axis.
4. For each roll, cut to desired width, then cut twice within each piece keeping 3 braids attached. Mini-braid and fold-and-roll from top to bottom (see Peaceful Cuisine's video).
5. Brush rolls with vegan "egg" wash (1 tbsp plant-based milk + 1 tbsp sweetener, e.g., soy milk and maple syrup).
6. Bake at 180°C for ~30-40 min. (with brown rice flour I used it took ~40min.) until golden and cooked through.
