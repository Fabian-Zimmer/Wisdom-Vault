**Storage:** All dressings keep in a sealed jar in the fridge. Shake or whisk before serving. Oil may solidify when cold, s set jar in hot water to loosen. All dressings thicken as they rest; thin with water, lemon juice, or aquafaba as needed.

## Red Wine Vinaigrette
*Yields ~180g. Pairs with: mixed greens, arugula, sturdy greens like kale. Also great in lentil or pasta salads.*

### Ingredients
| Amount          | Name                | Prep                          |
| --------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 2.5 tbsp        | Red wine vinegar    |                               |
| 1 tbsp          | Lemon juice         | Freshly squeezed              |
| 2-3 tsp         | Dijon mustard       |                               |
| 2-3 tsp         | Maple syrup         | Or agave                      |
| 2               | Garlic cloves       | Grated or finely minced       |
| 1/2-1 tsp       | Dried oregano       |                               |
| 1/4 tsp         | Red pepper flakes   | Optional                      |
| 84g (1/4c+2tbsp)| Extra virgin olive oil |                            |
| 1/2 tsp         | Salt                | Plus more to taste            |
|                 | Black pepper        | Freshly cracked               |

### Instructions
Add all ingredients to a sealed jar and shake vigorously until emulsified. Taste and adjust — more mustard for tang, more maple syrup for sweetness, more lemon or vinegar for acidity. Keeps up to 2 weeks.

## Creamy Balsamic Vinaigrette
*Yields ~175g. Best with sturdier, slightly bitter lettuces — romaine, Little Gem, shaved Brussels sprouts, radicchio, endive, tomato or cucumber salads. Also great drizzled over a fruit and vegan cheese plate.*

### Ingredients
| Amount          | Name                | Prep                          |
| --------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 60g (1/4 cup)   | Aged balsamic vinegar | Use a quality Modena IGP    |
| 1 tbsp          | Dijon mustard       |                               |
| 2               | Garlic cloves       | Minced or grated              |
| 1 small         | Shallot             | Roughly chopped               |
| 1.5-3 tsp       | Maple syrup         | Or cane sugar                 |
| 84g (1/4c+2tbsp)| Extra virgin olive oil |                            |
| 1/2 tsp         | Fine sea salt       | Plus more to taste            |
|                 | Black pepper        | Freshly cracked, generous     |

### Instructions
In the food processor (small bowl, knife blade), blend vinegar, mustard, garlic, shallot, salt, pepper, and maple syrup (1.5 tsp) until smooth. With the motor running, stream in the olive oil (84g) until emulsified. Taste and adjust sweetness and seasoning. Keeps 10-14 days.

## Sesame-Miso Dressing
*Yields ~170g. Pairs with: any cabbage slaw, shaved Brussels sprouts, shredded kale, crisp lettuces, cucumber or radish salads. Also great on noodle salads.*

### Ingredients
| Amount          | Name                    | Prep                              |
| --------------- | ----------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| 1 tbsp (17g)    | White miso              |                                   |
| 2.5 tbsp (37g)  | Unseasoned rice vinegar |                                   |
| 3               | Garlic cloves           | Grated or finely minced           |
| 1 inch piece    | Fresh ginger            | Peeled and grated                 |
| 1.5 tbsp (30g)  | Maple syrup             | Or agave                          |
| 2 tsp           | Dijon mustard           |                                   |
| 2 tsp           | Soy sauce               | Or tamari for GF                  |
| 2.5 tbsp (35g)  | Sunflower or neutral oil|                                   |
| 1.5 tbsp (21g)  | Toasted sesame oil      |                                   |

### Instructions
Whisk miso and rice vinegar in a jar until smooth with no large clumps. Add garlic, ginger, maple syrup, mustard, soy sauce, and both oils. Seal and shake vigorously until emulsified, or whisk in a bowl streaming in the oils. Taste and adjust. For a completely smooth dressing, blend in the food processor (small bowl, knife blade). Keeps 5-6 days.

## Lemon Tahini Dressing
*Yields ~230g. Pairs with: hearty greens (kale, cabbage), arugula, crisp lettuces (romaine, Little Gem, butter lettuce). Also great on grain bowls and wraps.*

### Ingredients
| Amount          | Name                | Prep                          |
| --------------- | ------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 60ml (1/4 cup)  | Lemon juice         | Freshly squeezed              |
| 1 tbsp (15ml)   | Apple cider vinegar |                               |
| 56g (1/4 cup)   | Tahini              | Well-stirred                  |
| 2 tsp           | Dijon mustard       |                               |
| 1 fat           | Garlic clove        | Grated or very finely minced  |
| 1 tbsp (20g)    | Maple syrup         |                               |
| 1/4 tsp         | Salt                | Plus more to taste            |
|                 | Black pepper        | Freshly cracked               |
| 56g (1/4 cup)   | Extra virgin olive oil |                            |
|                 | Cold water          | As needed to thin             |

### Instructions
In a jar or bowl, whisk together lemon juice, vinegar, tahini, mustard, garlic, maple syrup, salt, and pepper. Stream in the olive oil while whisking until emulsified. If too thick, whisk in cold water one tbsp at a time. Taste and adjust. Keeps 7-10 days. Will thicken in the fridge — thin with cold water before serving.

## Basil-Jalapeño Dressing
*Yields ~360g. Best with crisp lettuces (romaine, Little Gem, iceberg), shredded kale or cabbage, cucumber or snap pea salads. Also works as a sandwich spread or wrap drizzle.*

### Ingredients
| Amount          | Name                | Prep                                                                   |
| --------------- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 95g (2/3 cup)   | Raw cashews         | Soaked in boiling water 10-15 min., drained; or overnight in cool water|
| 1-2             | Jalapeño peppers    | Roughly chopped; start with 1, taste, add more for heat                |
| 24-32g (1.5-2 cups) | Fresh basil leaves | Packed; or substitute flat-leaf parsley or dill                    |
| 45-60ml (3-4 tbsp) | Lemon juice      | Freshly squeezed                                                       |
| 90ml (1/4c+2tbsp)| Water              |                                                                        |
| 60ml (1/4 cup)  | Aquafaba            | Liquid from a can of chickpeas                                         |
| 2 tbsp (28g)    | Extra virgin olive oil | Optional; omit for lighter, frothier dressing                      |
|                 | Salt and black pepper | To taste                                                             |

### Instructions
In the food processor (small bowl, knife blade), add aquafaba first and blend until frothy. Add drained cashews and all remaining ingredients. Blend for several minutes, scraping down the sides, until completely smooth and creamy. Taste and adjust — more lemon for tang, more jalapeño for heat. Keeps 5-7 days. Thickens in the fridge; thin with a splash of water, aquafaba, or lemon juice.

## Citrus-Date Dressing
*Yields ~275g. Pairs with peppery and earthy greens — arugula, Swiss chard, endive, thinly shaved fennel and raw beets. Salad idea: endive + shaved fennel + shaved raw beets + citrus segments + roasted pistachios; add lentils and avocado to make it filling.*

### Ingredients
| Amount          | Name                      | Prep                                              |
| --------------- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 4               | Medjool dates             | Soaked in warm water 10 min. if not soft, then drained |
| 84g (1/4c+2tbsp)| Extra virgin olive oil    |                                                   |
| 2 tsp           | Lemon zest                | From ~2 lemons                                    |
| 80ml (1/3 cup)  | Lemon juice               | Freshly squeezed, ~2 lemons                       |
| 1-2 tsp         | Orange zest               |                                                   |
| 30ml (2 tbsp)   | Orange juice              | Freshly squeezed                                  |
| 2 tsp           | Dijon or whole-grain mustard | Plus more to taste                             |
| 1 tsp           | Ground cumin              |                                                   |
| 1 tsp           | Sweet, hot, or smoked paprika |                                               |
| 1/2 tsp         | Ground coriander          |                                                   |
| 1/2-3/4 tsp     | Fine sea salt             |                                                   |
|                 | Black pepper              | Freshly ground                                    |
|                 | Water                     | To thin as needed                                 |

### Instructions
In the food processor (small bowl, knife blade), blend dates, olive oil, lemon zest and juice, 1 tsp of the orange zest, orange juice, mustard, cumin, paprika, coriander, 1/2 tsp salt, and pepper until smooth, scraping down the sides as needed. With the motor running, drizzle in water 1 tbsp at a time to reach desired consistency. Taste and adjust — more mustard for tang, more orange zest for floral sweetness, more salt if needed. Keeps up to 2 weeks. Thickens in the fridge; thin with lemon juice and water.
