## Ingredients

### Hummus
| Amount  | Name             | Prep                                                                                     |
| ------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 300g    | Cooked chickpeas | From ~125g dried and soaked overnight or 8h then pressure cooked, or pre-cooked from jar |
| 10g     | Fresh ginger     | Peeled and finely grated                                                                 |
| 1 tbsp  | Olive oil        |                                                                                          |
| 1 tbsp  | Tahini           |                                                                                          |
| 1 small | Garlic clove     | Crushed/minced                                                                           |
| 1       | Lemon            | Zest finely grated (~1/2 tbsp), then juiced (~1.5 tbsp)                                  |
| 2 tbsp  | Ice-cold water   |                                                                                          |
| 3/4 tsp | Flaked sea salt  |                                                                                          |

### Fried Aromatics
| Amount  | Name                | Prep                       |
| ------- | ------------------- | -------------------------- |
| 90ml    | Olive oil           |                            |
| 3       | (Long) red chillies | Deseeded and finely sliced |
| 3 large | Garlic cloves       | Thinly sliced              |
| 15g     | Fresh ginger        | Peeled and julienned       |
| 2       | Cinnamon sticks     |                            |
| 15g     | Coriander stalks    | Cut into 4cm lengths       |
| 2 tbsp  | Coriander leaves    | Optional, to serve         |
|         | Flaked sea salt     | To sprinkle                |
|         | Bread               | To serve                   |

## Instructions
**Note:** The hummus can be made the day before and refrigerated. Fry the aromatics on the day of serving to keep them crisp.

0. Needs prepared chickpeas (see above in ingredients prep notes).
1. Blend all hummus ingredients in the food processor (large bowl and knife blade) — chickpeas (300g), ginger (10g), olive oil (1 tbsp), tahini (1 tbsp), garlic (1 clove), lemon zest (~1/2 tbsp) and lemon juice (~1.5 tbsp) from lemon (1), ice-cold water (2 tbsp), and flaked salt (3/4 tsp) — until smooth, scraping down the sides as needed.
2. For the fried aromatics, heat olive oil (90ml) in a large frying pan over medium heat. Fry the chillies (3), garlic (3 cloves), ginger (15g), and cinnamon sticks (2) for 4-5 min., stirring occasionally, until the garlic is just turning golden. Add coriander stalks (15g) and fry for 1 more min. until the garlic is light golden-brown and aromatic. Sprinkle with salt (ideally flaked).
3. Spoon the hummus onto a large plate and create a shallow well in the centre with the back of a spoon. Pour the aromatics and its oil into the well (top with coriander leaves if using).
