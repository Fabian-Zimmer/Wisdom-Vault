## Ingredients
| Amount | Name               | Prep                                                                               |
| ------ | ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|        | Olive oil          | For cooking                                                                        |
| 2      | Onions             | Finely chopped                                                                     |
| 4-5    | Garlic cloves      | Sliced                                                                             |
| 1      | Long red jalapeño  | Finely chopped                                                                     |
| 2      | Red bell peppers   | Cube-chopped                                                                       |
|        | Cherry tomatoes    | Halved                                                                             |
| 2 cans | Whole-tomato cans  | Better quality tomatoes than pureé cans, stamp/squash later during cooking process |
|        | Black olives       | Jar, drained and sliced                                                            |
| 1 tbsp | Aceto balsamico    |                                                                                    |
|        | Soya cuisine       | Dash                                                                               |
|        | Fresh basil leaves | Torn                                                                               |
|        | Pasta              | As needed                                                                          |
|        | Parmesan           | Grated, to serve                                                                   |
|        | Salt               | To taste                                                                           |

## Instructions
1. In a pot with olive oil, fry onions on medium heat until softened, ~5 min. Add garlic and jalapeño and cook for another 2 min.
2. Add bell peppers and cook until slightly softened, ~3-4 min.
3. Add cherry tomatoes and cook for a couple of minutes until they begin to break down.
4. Pour in whole-tomatos (2 cans) and squish them into purée. Stir well, and bring to a gentle simmer. Add balsamic vinegar (1 tbsp) and a dash of soya cuisine. Season with salt to taste.
5. Simmer on low heat for ~20 min., stirring occasionally, until the sauce has thickened and deepened in flavour.
6. Meanwhile, cook pasta in salted water according to package instructions. Drain, reserving a little pasta water.
7. Stir olives into the sauce. Add the cooked pasta and toss to coat, adding a splash of pasta water if needed.
8. Turn off the heat and stir in torn basil leaves. Serve topped with grated parmesan.
