## Instructions

0. Soak cashew nuts in boiling water for ~30min.
1. Make carrot purée: peel and roughly chop carrots until you fill a heaping 1/2 cup, then cook them in water for ~3-5min until just soft, then purée in food processor (small bowl and knife) or (immersion) blender, then measure again to only get 1/2 cup (important, since they may have increased in volume during cooking).
2. Make the flax eggs: mix flaxseed powder (2 tbsp) with water (6 tbsp) and leave to rest until gel-like, ~5 min.
3. Preheat oven to 175°C. Line a 22cm/9" round baking form with parchment paper.
4. In a large bowl, combine all wet ingredients: flax eggs, purée (1 cup), date syrup (1/3 cup), plant milk (1/4 cup), apple cider vinegar (1 tbsp), vanilla extract (1.5 tsp), and grated carrots (1.5 cups). Mix well.
5. In a separate bowl, combine all dry ingredients: oat flour (2 + 1/4), almond flour (1 + 1/2), baking powder (2 tsp), baking soda (1/2 tsp), cinnamon (1.5 tsp), cloves (1/8 tsp), nutmeg (1/4 tsp), and salt if using. Mix well.
6. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry and mix until a thick batter forms. Pour into the prepared pan and smooth the top.
7. Bake for 25-30 min., or until a toothpick inserted in the centre comes out clean. Transfer to a cooling rack and leave to cool completely.
8. Make the frosting: blend soaked cashews (1.5 cups), coconut milk or cream (1/2 cup), lemon juice (2 tbsp, ~1/2 big yellow lemon), dates (6), and vanilla extract (1 tsp) in the food processor (large bowl and knife blade) until completely smooth. If too thin, refrigerate until the cake is ready.
9. Once the cake is fully cooled, smooth the frosting over the top. Sprinkle with cinnamon and chopped walnuts.
