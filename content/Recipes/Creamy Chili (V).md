## Ingredients

### Homemade Chili Powder (optional; makes ~12 tbsp, enough for 3 batches; store rest up to 6 months)
| Amount    | Name                  | Prep                                                              |
| --------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 4         | Ancho peppers         | Stems removed, seeds and membranes scraped out                    |
| 4         | Guajillo peppers      | Stems removed, seeds and membranes scraped out                    |
| 2-5       | Chiles de arbol       | 0 for mild, 2 for moderate, 4-5 for very spicy                   |
| 2 tbsp    | Cumin seeds           | Toasted whole                                                     |
| 1/2 tbsp  | Coriander seeds       | Optional, toasted whole                                           |
| 1 tbsp    | Mexican oregano       | Or 2 tsp regular oregano or marjoram                             |
| 2 tsp     | Garlic powder         |                                                                   |
| 2 tsp     | Onion powder          |                                                                   |
| 1 tsp     | Smoked paprika        |                                                                   |
| 1/4 tsp   | Ground cinnamon       |                                                                   |
**Instructions:**
Toast the dried chillies in a dry heavy pan over medium heat: larger ones ~1 min. per side, chiles de arbol ~30 sec. per side. Watch closely; don't let them blacken or they'll turn bitter. Toast cumin and coriander seeds separately until fragrant, ~45-60 sec. Cool completely, then grind chillies and whole spices in a spice grinder or food processor until pulverized. Add the pre-ground spices and blend again to a fine powder. Store in an airtight jar in a cool, dry place for up to 6 months.

**Substitute:**
Store-bought works too: use ancho chilli powder (just ground ancho peppers, more flavourful than standard chilli powder). With store-bought, you may need less maple syrup and lime juice to balance since it's less bitter, and add extra jalapeños if you want heat.

## Instructions
**Notes:** 
- For a gentler spice version: use 1 jalapeño (membranes removed), 1 chipotle pepper + 1 tsp adobo sauce, and mild chilli powder. 
- The chili always tastes better the next day as the spices meld. 
- Stores in the fridge for 5-6 days; freezes well for 3-4 months.

1. Heat olive oil (3 tbsp) in a large Dutch oven or a large heavy-bottomed pot over medium-high. Add onion with a few pinches of salt and cook for ~10 min., stirring occasionally, until nicely golden. Add a splash of water if it starts to burn.
2. Add garlic (6 cloves), jalapeños (1-2), and tomato paste (2 tbsp). Cook for 2-3 min., stirring frequently, until the paste darkens. Add a splash of water if it dries out.
3. Stir in chilli powder (4 tbsp), cumin (1 tbsp), paprika (1.5 tsp), and oregano (1 tbsp). Stir vigorously for 30 seconds to toast the spices.
4. Pour in red wine (180ml) to deglaze, scraping up any browned bits. Simmer rapidly for 3-4 min. until the alcohol has cooked off and the wine is jammy.
5. Add vegetable broth (480ml), pinto beans (850g), navy beans (425g), cocoa powder (2 tbsp), bay leaves (2), soy sauce (1.5 tbsp), the chipotle peppers (2) and adobo sauce (1 tbsp), crushed tomatoes (800g) with their juices, salt (1.5 tsp), black pepper (to taste), and maple syrup (1 tbsp). Stir well.
6. Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to the gentlest possible simmer. Do not boil or rapidly simmer — it will break down the beans. Cook for 1.5-2h, stirring every 10 min., until thick, velvety, and flavours have melded. Discard bay leaves.
7. If using, stir in masa harina (3 tbsp) and simmer for 3 min. to thicken further.
8. Stir in lime juice (1-1.5 tbsp), vinegar (1 tsp), and chopped coriander leaves incl. tender stems (1 cup). Taste and adjust — add more lime for tang, up to 1 more tbsp maple syrup to balance bitterness, and salt and pepper as needed.
9. Serve with toppings of choice. Also great alongside warm corn tortillas, rice, or cornbread.
