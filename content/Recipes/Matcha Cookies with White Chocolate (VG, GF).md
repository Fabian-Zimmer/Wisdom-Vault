## Ingredients

**Note:** These make ~12 cookies.

| Amount               | Name                  | Prep/Notes                                                                      |
| -------------------- | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 225g (2 cups packed) | Almond flour          | Ideally ultra-fine; grind/blend to finer consistency if using almond meal       |
| 1 tbsp               | Matcha powder         | Up to 1 tbsp extra for stronger flavour, but don't overdo as it dries the dough |
| 1/2 tsp              | Baking powder         | Or 1/4 tsp baking soda                                                          |
| 50g (~45ml)          | Maple syrup           | Or agave; for crispier cookies replace 1 tbsp with 1 tbsp water                 |
| 50g (~55ml)          | Coconut oil           | Melted; plus a little extra to grease hands                                     |
| 1/2 tsp              | Vanilla extract       | Optional                                                                        |
| ~40g                 | Vegan white chocolate | Finely chopped into small pieces, to fold into dough                            |

## Instructions

**Note:** These are softer than regular cookies, like healthy vegan shortbread with a strong almond flavour, so don't overbake.

1. Preheat oven to 160°C. Line a baking tray with parchment paper.
2. Finely chop the white chocolate (~40g) into very small pieces and set aside.
3. In a mixing bowl, combine almond flour (225g), matcha (1 tbsp), and baking powder (1/2 tsp). Stir in melted coconut oil (50g), maple syrup (50g), and vanilla extract if using, until a sticky green dough forms. Taste and adjust matcha if desired. Fold in the chopped white chocolate.
4. Lightly grease hands with coconut oil. Scoop ~2 tbsp of dough per cookie, roll into a ball, and place on the tray with a thumb's width between each. Flatten slightly with your palm, keeping a slight dome in the centre for a moist middle and crispy edges.
5. Bake for 15-18 min. until slightly golden on the sides and just set on top. Cool on the tray for 5 min., then transfer to a cooling rack to cool completely.
