> [!warning] Needs high-powered blender! Cannot be done in food processor!

## Ingredients

### Base
| Amount  | Name          | Prep                                          |
| ------- | ------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| 1 cup   | Raw cashews   | Soaked in boiling water 20 min., then drained |
| 150g    | Coconut cream | (see notes and instructions below)            |
| 1-2 tsp | Lactic acid   |                                               |
| 1/2 tsp | Salt          | To taste                                      |

### Optional Mix-ins (pick one or combine)
| Amount       | Name                  | Prep                              |
| ------------ | --------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| 15g          | Dried strawberries    | For strawberry cream cheese       |
| 1            | Jalapeño              | Finely diced; + 1/2 tsp garlic powder |
| 5-6 slices   | Dill pickles          | Finely diced; + 1 tsp fresh dill + 1/2 tsp garlic powder |

## Instructions
**Notes:** 
- Refrigerate coconut milk can overnight, so that cream part solidifies on top of can and easily scoops out.
- Store in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 1 week.

1. Soak cashews (1 cup) in boiling water for ~20 min., then drain. Discard the soaking water.
2. Separate the thick coconut cream (150g) from the rest in the can. Set the water aside — it can be used to thin the mixture slightly later if needed.
3. Add drained cashews, coconut cream, lactic acid (1-2 tsp, *but add bit by bit and taste in between*), and salt (1/2 tsp) to a high-speed blender. Blend until completely smooth.
4. Taste and adjust lactic acid and salt as needed. Fold in any mix-ins at this stage.
5. Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate for at least 6h, ideally overnight, to thicken.
