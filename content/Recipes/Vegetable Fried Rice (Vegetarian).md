## Recommended Equipment
- 1 large wok or deep frying pan — main cooking vessel
- 1 large pot or second wok — for the final split stir-fry stage; can be preheated and used to keep half the food warm while you mix
- 1 smaller pot — for cooking peas

## Ingredients

### Rice & Vegetables
| Amount         | Name               | Prep                                              |
| -------------- | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------- |
| 400g           | Rice               |                                                   |
| 1 small        | Cauliflower        | Cut into florets                                  |
| 2 small/medium | Broccoli           | Cut into florets                                  |
| 1              | Red bell pepper    | Chopped                                           |
| 1              | Yellow bell pepper | Chopped                                           |
| 3 medium       | Carrots            | Chopped                                           |
| 1 coloured cup | Peas               |                                                   |
|                | Spring onions      | Sliced; white and green parts separated           |
| 5              | Green chillies     | Chopped                                           |
|                | Garlic             | Chopped                                           |
|                | Ginger             | As much as garlic, chopped                        |
| 2 packs        | Paneer             | Fried in thick slabs, cooled, then cut into cubes |
|                | Neutral oil        | For cookingg                                      |

### Sauce
| Amount  | Name               | Prep |
| ------- | ------------------ | ---- |
| 3 tbsp  | Light soy sauce    |      |
| 3 tbsp  | Dark soy sauce     |      |
| 3 tbsp  | Rice wine vinegar  |      |
| 2 tbsp  | Sesame oil         |      |
| 2 tbsp  | Sriracha           |      |
| 1 tbsp  | Crispy chilli oil  |      |

## Instructions
**Note:** Do mise-en-place by pre-chopping all ingredients before you start cooking, as it can go quite fast with the frying and cooking stages.

1. Cook rice (400g) first so it has time to cool and dry out — ideally spread on a tray.
2. In a separate smaller pot, cook the peas.
3. Pre-heat the wok and add oil, then fry the paneer in medium-thick slabs (2-3 at a time) until golden-brown on both sides. Set aside to cool, then cut into cubes.
4. On medium-high heat: in the wok with a little oil, fry garlic, ginger, green chillies, and the white parts of the spring onions until fragrant.
5. Reduce to medium heat or lower: add cauliflower, broccoli, bell peppers, carrots, and the green parts of the spring onions to the wok. Deglaze with a bit of water if necessary, and cook until slightly tender. Add peas when done.
6. While the vegetables are cooking, mix all sauce ingredients together in a bowl. Pour over the paneer cubes and let them soak.
7. Split the cooked vegetables equally between the wok and another large pot (preheated and oiled). Then split the food equally:
	1. Split the vegetables from wok to the other pot,
	2. Then add equally the cooled, dried rice and the paneer with the sauce. 
8. Stir-fry on medium-high heat one last time, tossing everything together until well combined and heated through.
