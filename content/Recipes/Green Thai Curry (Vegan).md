## Ingredients

| Amount         | Name                              | Prep                          |
| -------------- | --------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| 1 cup          | Jasmine rice                      |                               |
| ~350-400g      | Firm tofu                         | Cut in small rectangles       |
|                | Sunflower oil                     | For frying                    |
| 2 cans         | Coconut milk                      | 400ml each                    |
| 685ml          | Water                             |                               |
| 1-2            | Red bell pepper                   | Sliced finely                 |
| 1 medium-sized | Eggplant or Zucchini              | Sliced finely                 |
| 1 medium       | Broccoli                          | Sliced finely                 |
| 4-5            | Garlic cloves                     | Thinly sliced                 |
|                | Ginger                            | Same volume as garlic, grated |
| 2 stalks       | Lemongrass                        | Thinly sliced                 |
| 4              | Green chillies                    | Thinly sliced                 |
| ~280g          | Thai green/yellow/red curry paste |                               |
| 2 tsp          | Coconut/brown sugar               |                               |
| 1/2            | Lime                              | Juiced                        |
| 1 tsp          | Salt                              |                               |
| ~10            | Thai Basil leaves                 |                               |

## Instructions

1. Cook jasmine rice (1 cup).
2. Fry firm tofu (~350-400g, cut in big slabs) in a wok with sunflower oil until golden. Set aside to cool and then cut in desired piece size.
3. In the wok, dissolve the solid coconut cream from coconut milk cans (2 cans, 400ml each).
4. Add thinly sliced garlic (4-5 cloves), grated ginger (same volume as garlic), thinly sliced lemongrass (2 stalks), thinly sliced green chillies (4), Thai green/yellow/red curry paste (~280g), coconut/brown sugar (2 tsp), and lime juice (1/2 lime). Cook for ~5 minutes until fragrant.
5. Add the remaining liquid coconut milk from the cans and water (685ml). Bring to a boil.
6. Add eggplant or zucchini (1 medium-sized, thinly sliced), red bell pepper (1-2, thinly sliced), and salt (1 tsp). Let cook for 7-10 minutes until vegetables are tender/al dente.
7. Add broccoli (1 medium, small florets) and fried tofu pieces. Salt more to taste.
8. Add Thai Basil leaves (~10), turn off heat, and let rest for approximately 10 minutes.
9. Serve with jasmine rice.
