## Ingredients
| Amount  | Name            | Prep                                                                  |
| ------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 110g    | Pastry flour    | Original: 100g                                                        |
| 20g     | Tapioca starch  | For crunchiness; or replace with 20g more pastry flour                |
| 20g     | Cane sugar      | Original: 40g                                                         |
| 50g     | Neutral oil     |                                                                       |
| 30g     | Coconut milk    | Original: 20g                                                         |
| 1 tbsp  | Tea leaves      | E.g. earl grey, Indian chai, ground to fine powder. Or matcha powder. |
| 1/2 tsp | Vanilla extract |                                                                       |
| 1/2 tsp | Baking powder   |                                                                       |

## Instructions
**Note:** The original recipe uses 100g pastry flour, 40g cane sugar, and 20g coconut milk. Sugar has been reduced to 20g and compensated with extra coconut milk (30g) and flour (110g) for balance — adjust back towards the original if you find the cookies too dry or not sweet enough.

1. Mix all ingredients together — pastry flour (110g), tapioca starch or more pastry flour (20g), cane sugar (20g), neutral oil (50g), coconut milk (30g), ground tea leaves (1 tbsp), vanilla extract (1/2 tsp), and baking powder (1/2 tsp) — until a dough forms.
2. Roll the dough into a cylinder using plastic wrap — wrap it tight and use the wrap to help roll and shape it evenly.
3. Chill in the freezer for 30 min. to firm up for cleaner cutting.
4. Cut into evenly thick disks.
5. Bake at 180°C for 15-20 min.
