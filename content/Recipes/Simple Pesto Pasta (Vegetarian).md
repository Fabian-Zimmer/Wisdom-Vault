## Ingredients
| Amount              | Name                         | Prep             |
| ------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------- |
| 2 big               | Broccoli                     | Cut into florets |
| 2                   | Bell peppers (yellow/red)    | Chopped          |
|                     | Olive oil                    | For cooking      |
| 2                   | Onions                       | Semi-rough cubes |
| 3                   | Garlic cloves                | Sliced           |
| 8 packs (~15g each) | Fresh basil leaves           |                  |
| 1 big               | Garlic clove                 | For pesto        |
| 45g                 | Olive oil                    | For pesto        |
| 1 tbsp              | Parmesan                     | Grated           |
| ~20g                | Pine nuts                    |                  |
| 185g                | Pre-made pesto alla Genovese |                  |
|                     | Pasta                        | As needed        |

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven. Roast broccoli florets (2 big) and chopped bell peppers (2) until cooked through and slightly charred.
2. Meanwhile, in a wok with olive oil, fry garlic (3 cloves, sliced) and onions (2) until softened and lightly golden.
3. Blend the pesto: basil leaves (8 packs), garlic (1 big clove), olive oil (45g), grated parmesan (1 tbsp), and pine nuts (~20g). Add the pre-made pesto alla Genovese (185g) and blend together. Finish with a small steel cup of water to loosen to desired consistency.
4. Cook pasta according to package instructions. Drain, reserving a little pasta water.
5. Add the roasted vegetables to the wok with the onions and garlic. Add the pasta and pour over the pesto sauce. Toss everything together, adding a splash of reserved pasta water if needed to coat well.
