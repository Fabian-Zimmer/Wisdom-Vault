## Ingredients
| Amount                | Name                        | Prep                                                  |
| --------------------- | --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| 200g (1 cup)          | Dry chickpeas               | Soaked overnight or 8h, should be ~400g after soaking |
| 180ml (3/4 cup)       | Water                       |                                                       |
| 1/2 tbsp              | Olive oil                   |                                                       |
| 25g (1/4 cup)         | Flaxseed powder/meal        |                                                       |
| 40g (4 tbsp)          | Psyllium husk powder        |                                                       |
| 1+1/4 tsp             | Baking powder               |                                                       |
| 1 tsp                 | Baking soda                 |                                                       |
| 1/2 tsp               | Salt                        |                                                       |
| 16g (1+1/2 tbsp)      | Vinegar or lemon/lime juice |                                                       |
|                       | Seeds of choice             | Poppy, flax, pumpkin, etc. — for topping              |
| ~2 big cloves         | Garlic                      | Grated or minced                                      |
| 2-3 tsp               | Mixed herbs                 | To taste. E.g., tijm, oregano, basilicum              |

## Instructions
1. Soak dry chickpeas (200g) overnight or for 8h, then drain.
   !Note: these are only soaked, not cooked! It still blends well even with staff mixer.
2. Blend the soaked chickpeas with water (180ml) and olive oil (1/2 tbsp) until smooth.
3. Add flaxseed powder (25g), psyllium husk powder (40g), baking powder (1+1/4 tsp), baking soda (1 tsp), and salt (1/2 tsp). Also add grated/minced garlic (~2 cloves), and mixed herbs (2-3 tsp, e.g., tijm, oregano, basilicum). Blend well — especially the baking soda, which should be fully incorporated before the next step to trap the reaction bubbles in the dough.
4. Add vinegar or lemon/lime juice (1+1/2 or ~16g) and blend briefly to combine.
5. Pour the mixture onto a parchment-lined oven tray. Slightly wet your hands and shape into an oval loaf without kneading — just push and form. Sprinkle seeds (e.g., flax, poppy, pumpkin, or others) evenly across the top, then press them into the dough a bit.
6. Bake at 185°C for 60-70 min on the same tray where you shaped it, no meed to transfer to a loaf baking form. 
7. Leave to cool completely for at least 1h before slicing — cutting too early will cause the bread to be destroyed.
