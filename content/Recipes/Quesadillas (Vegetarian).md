## Ingredients
| Amount              | Name                  | Prep                                          |
| ------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| 2                   | Onions                | Finely chopped into rings                     |
| 2                   | Green bell peppers    | Finely cube-chopped                           |
| 4 big               | Garlic cloves         | Sliced                                        |
| 150g                | Tomato paste          | Or mix of chopped cherry tomatoes and purée   |
| 300g net (1 can)    | Corn                  | Drained                                       |
| 400g net (1 can)    | Black beans           | Drained and rinsed                            |
| 1 tsp               | Red chilli powder     |                                               |
| 1 tsp               | Garam masala          |                                               |
| 1/2 tsp             | Turmeric              |                                               |
|                     | Olive oil             | For cooking                                   |
|                     | Salt                  | To taste                                      |
|                     | Tortillas             | For serving                                   |
|                     | Yoghurt               | For dip                                       |
|                     | Lime                  | Juiced, for dip                               |
|                     | Fresh coriander       | Chopped, for dip                              |

## Instructions

1. Mix the dip: combine yoghurt with a squeeze of lime juice and a handful of chopped coriander. Season with salt to taste and set aside in the fridge.
2. In a pan with olive oil, fry onions on medium heat until softened and lightly golden, ~5 min. Add garlic and cook for another 2 min.
3. Add green bell peppers and cook until softened, ~3-4 min.
4. Add red chilli powder (1 tsp), garam masala (1 tsp), and turmeric (1/2 tsp). Stir well and cook for a minute until fragrant.
5. Stir in tomato paste (150g) and cook for 2-3 min.
6. Add corn (300g, drained) and black beans (400g, drained and rinsed). Stir to combine and simmer on low heat for ~5 min. until heated through. Season with salt to taste.
7. Warm tortillas in a dry pan. Spoon the filling onto one half of each tortilla, fold over, and press down. Cook in the dry pan for 1-2 min. each side until lightly crispy. Serve with the dip on the side.
