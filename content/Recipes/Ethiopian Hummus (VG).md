## Ingredients

### Hummus
| Amount  | Name                          | Prep                                                                                            |
| ------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 200g    | Cooked chickpeas              | From ~75g dried, soaked 6-8h (or overnight) then pressure cooked for 3-4 whistles on high heat. |
| 30g     | Sunflower seeds               | Toasted                                                                                         |
| 1 tbsp  | Olive oil                     |                                                                                                 |
| 1       | Lemon                         | Zested, then juiced                                                                             |
| 1/3 tsp | Jalapeño                      | Or more to taste                                                                                |
| 1       | Garlic clove                  |                                                                                                 |
| 1/3 tsp | Salt                          | Or to taste                                                                                     |
| 1/2 tsp | Berbere spice                 | See below; more to sprinkle on top                                                              |
|         | Water                         | A little, to adjust consistency                                                                 |
|         | Whole toasted sunflower seeds | To garnish                                                                                      |

### Berbere Spice Mix (makes more than needed — stores well)
| Amount    | Name                  | Prep                                     |
| --------- | --------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| 1 tbsp    | Dried chilli peppers  | Whole or powder                          |
| 2 tbsp    | Paprika powder        |                                          |
| 1 tsp     | Salt                  |                                          |
| 1 tsp     | Coriander             | Whole seeds or powder                    |
| 1/2 tsp   | Dried ginger          |                                          |
| 1/2 tsp   | Fenugreek             | Whole seeds or powder                    |
| 1/2 tsp   | Cardamom              | Whole pods or powder                     |
| 1/2 tsp   | Nutmeg                | Whole, grated fresh; or powder           |
| 1/2 tsp   | Allspice              | Whole berries or powder                  |
| 1/4 tsp   | Cloves                | Whole or powder                          |

## Instructions
**Note:** For the berbere spice, ideally grind whole/raw spices in a spice grinder/blender for the best flavour — powder form works fine too. The berbere makes more than needed for this recipe; store the rest in a jar for future use.

1. Make the berbere spice: optionally toast whole spices briefly in a dry pan. Grind all ingredients except nutmeg in a spice grinder to a fine powder. Grate in the nutmeg and stir to combine. Set aside.
2. Toast sunflower seeds (30g) in a dry pan until aromatic/golden/few minutes. Reserve a small handful for garnish.
3. In the food processor (small bowl and knife blade), grind the toasted sunflower seeds (minus the reserved handful) to a fine powder.
4. Add chickpeas (200g), olive oil (1 tbsp), zest and juice from lemon (1), jalapeño (1/3 tsp), garlic (1 clove), salt (1/3 tsp), and berbere spice (1/2 tsp). Process until smooth, adding a little water as needed to reach desired consistency.
5. Spread onto a plate, sprinkle with a little more berbere spice and the reserved whole toasted sunflower seeds.
