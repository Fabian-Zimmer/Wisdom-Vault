## Ingredients

**Note:** Bread flour should be wheat-based and have high protein percentage, e.g., ~13-14%, for strong gluten networks.

### Tangzhong
| Amount          | Name                     | Prep |
| --------------- | ------------------------ | ---- |
| 2 tbsp          | High protein bread flour |      |
| 120ml (1/2 cup) | Water                    |      |

### Dough
| Amount              | Name              | Prep                         |
| ------------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 240ml (1 cup)       | Soy milk          | Room temp. or slightly warm  |
| 2.25 tsp (1 packet) | Active dry yeast  |                              |
| 3 tbsp              | Granulated sugar  |                              |
| 1 tsp               | Salt              |                              |
| 480g (4 cups)       | Bread flour       | Plus extra for kneading      |
| 55g (1/4 cup)       | Vegan butter      | Softened, cut into pieces    |
| Tangzhong           | See above         | Cooled                       |

### Vegan Egg Wash
| Amount       | Name              | Prep                  |
| ------------ | ----------------- | --------------------- |
| 1 tbsp       | Soy milk          |                       |
| 1 tbsp       | Granulated sugar  |                       |

## Instructions
**Note:** Use a 9×5" loaf tin if possible. A stand mixer is strongly recommended — kneading by hand takes 10-12 min. and requires more effort. Go by volume rather than time for the rises, as room temperature affects speed significantly.

### Tangzhong
1. In a small pan, whisk together bread flour (2 tbsp) and water (120ml) until smooth. Heat over medium-high, stirring constantly, until thickened to a paste. Remove from heat and leave to cool.

### Dough
2. In a bowl, whisk yeast into lukewarm soy milk (240ml, warmed for example in microwave) and leave to proof for ~5 min. until foamy.
3. Add sugar (3 tbsp), salt (1 tsp), cooled tangzhong, bread flour (480g), and softened vegan butter (55g). Mix until a shaggy dough forms.
4. Knead on for 8-10 min. if using stand mixer, if by hand then longer, until smooth and elastic — the dough should bounce back when poked. Add extra flour, 2 tbsp at a time, if the dough is overly sticky, fully incorporating before adding more.
5. Shape into a ball and transfer to a lightly greased bowl. Cover with a damp towel or plastic wrap and leave to rise in a warm spot for ~1h, until doubled in size.

### Egg Wash
6. Mix soy milk (1 tbsp) and sugar (1 tbsp) in a small microwave-safe bowl. Microwave in 10-15 second bursts until bubbling and sugar has dissolved. Stir, cover loosely, and set aside to cool.

### Shaping
7. Punch down the risen dough and divide into 3 equal portions. Shape each into a ball, cover, and rest for ~10 min. to relax the gluten.
8. Roll each ball into a wide oval ~1cm thick. Fold both edges, along the long axis, in toward the centre overlapping slightly, then roll gently back to ~1cm thickness. Starting from the edge closest to you, roll tightly into a log. Pinch the seam to seal.
9. Place the 3 logs seam-side down into a greased loaf tin, evenly spaced. Cover and leave to rise for ~1h until just cresting over the top edge of the tin.

### Baking
10. Preheat oven to 175°C. Brush the top of the loaf evenly with the egg wash.
11. Bake for 30-35 min. until golden brown — internal temperature should reach 88-93°C. Tent loosely with foil if browning too quickly.
12. Cool in the tin for 10 min., then transfer to a wire rack. Leave to cool fully before slicing — at least 45 min. — otherwise the interior will be gummy.
