## Ingredients

**Base (makes ~200g nut butter min.):** use any combination of nuts totalling ~170g. Some examples:

| Combination      | Nuts                         | Character                     |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Almond-Cashew    | 120g almonds + 50g cashews   | Classic, mild, slightly sweet |
| Pure Cashew      | 170g cashews                 | Very creamy, naturally sweet  |
| Macadamia-Cashew | 120g macadamia + 50g cashews | Rich, buttery, indulgent      |
| Pure Almond      | 170g almonds                 | Earthy, slightly grainy       |
| Mixed            | Equal parts of any 2-3 nuts  | Experiment freely             |

| Amount       | Name                  | Prep                                        |
| ------------ | --------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| ~150g total  | Nuts of choice        | See combinations above                      |
| 10g          | Coconut oil           | Melted or soft                              |
| 20g+         | Vegan butter          | Add more for a softer, spreadable result    |

## Instructions
1. Add nuts (~170g) and melted coconut oil (10g) to a food processor. Blitz continuously, scraping down the sides as needed — this will take several minutes and go through a crumbly stage before turning smooth and buttery. Be patient.
2. Once smooth, add vegan butter (20g) and blitz again until fully incorporated. Taste and add more butter if you prefer a softer consistency.
3. Transfer to a jar and store in the fridge.

**Note (optional):** Roasting the nuts at 180°C for 8-10 min. before blending deepens the flavour and speeds up the blending process.
