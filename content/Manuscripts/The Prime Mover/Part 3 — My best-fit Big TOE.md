### Simplicity and parsimony.

The universe, as well as theory choice in the philosophy of science, seems to be guided by simplicity and parsimony. Why does it seem that simplicity is "baked into" reality and us like this?
When constructing a big TOE with the driver being consciousness evolution, or simply an evolutionary aspect in general, then this question can be answered by appealing to how evolution tends to optimally (and therefore minimally) explore the state and solution space it has access to, or which it is coupled to. Or it settles into an optimal state that uses the minimal resources it needs over time. (Is this how evolution works, parallels here on earth?)

### Us as learning blobs within a loving computer

MBT likens our portion of reality to data-streams that us IUOCs receive from a construction of consciousness akin to a computer. These data-streams are synchronised, so to speak, such that all of us that participate in this PMR are experiencing the same reality frame and hence can learn together. 

This entity that has part of it configured as a computer must be incredibly evolved and loving, because it has effectively eternally stable parts that give us the solidity of physics and causality that we experience. We can also compute things within our minds, but (at least for me) it is impossible to hold them stable and consistent for longer than a few seconds, because a thought will eventually pop in and disrupt whatever I'm trying to hold stable. 

Ultimately in this picture, there is nothing but us blobs of entropy-lowering and learning entities interfaced with and supported by the stable computing engine that gives us data-streams with unconditional love. All of the physicality and causality we experience is virtual and temporary. This has consequences for everything that we think is evil and terrible in the world. As long as your intent is one of trying to learn, care, and love, the actions and consequences you experience in this reality are not what really matters. One can literally make any example:
If your intention is to help and you send money to an organisation that does foreign aid as best as you can tell and understand, you should be evolving positively, even if it later turns out that the organisation was actually exploiting others and worsening the overall situation of human well-being. Because remember, the only aspects that "worsen" are attributed to the virtual data-streams, but the underlying computing bits are still loving and cooperating. We only interpret it from our PMR perspective to have worsened the overall situation, but if all IUOCs involved have evolved positively during this process, then the overall entropy of that portion of the LCS has in fact lowered and it has evolved. You can apply this to any catastrophe and disastrous consequences in the world. Only if our intentions were bad, evil, negative, etc., then our LCS portion would have de-evolved.
